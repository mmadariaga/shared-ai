**Complexity**: high

## Why

Apply recovery currently spends the same bounded retry pool on corrections that are already impossible under the worker's authorized scope, and it can repeat an unchanged diagnosis until the pool is exhausted. In the triggering `/sai-build` run, a Step 2 verification command asserted a wrapper inventory that only Step 3 creates, so all three recovery attempts were consumed without addressing the actual cause.

## What Changes

- Add one shared, phase-neutral non-clean-closure procedure to the command runner. After resolution, every failed result of any class, coordinator-disproved `completed` result, or `completed` result carrying `STOP reached? = yes` enters diagnosis. The coordinator selects exactly one of three mutually exclusive routing diagnoses: a worker-authored classified failure, a coordinator-authored contract/verification rejection, or a continuation/transport loss. Clean cancellation, `needs_input`, and pre-resolution failures remain outside this diagnosis route.
- Redefine the shared recovery budget as three mutually distinct coordinator diagnosis slots, with at most one same-worker recovery attempt per slot. A duplicate diagnosis stops recovery before dispatch and before exhaustion, even when an unused slot remains; an out-of-scope or unresolved cause spends zero. The runner keeps the segment boundary, changed-file union, veto precedence, and successor/re-entry rules unchanged.
- Update Apply's Known-False Report Recovery to branch by cause locus and carry the five-part coordinator diagnosis (`Reported`, `Evidence`, `Cause`, `Correction`, `Verification`). An out-of-scope plan defect may receive at most one bounded coordinator-only repair of the current Step's `implementation.md`; that repair may not edit production, test, interface, or verification-run files, execute verification as part of the repair, redefine scope, or reduce coverage. Independent coordinator verification remains separate and authoritative.
- Bind an unpassable RED or GREEN STOP to a failed worker outcome with `failure_class: blocking-contradiction` and concrete evidence. The `unrecoverable` boolean remains worker-evidence-driven: a worker sets it to `true` only when continuation is unsafe; otherwise the coordinator's Cause Locus may admit one new in-scope recovery diagnosis. The GREEN test/interface prohibition remains absolute, and the RED worker gains only the bounded same-worker recovery continuation permitted by the shared route; neither worker may force a pass by editing the other worker's prohibited surface.
- Preserve coordinator-owned, independent verification after every dispatch and recovery continuation. `/sai-build` inherits the shared behavior through the existing Apply phase adapter; `sai/commands/build/coordinator.md` is not modified and retains its composition-owned stops, resolution, re-entry, and fast-track behavior.

## Additional Notes

- The three diagnosis categories are routing diagnoses, not additions to the worker-authored `failure_class` vocabulary. `blocking-contradiction`, `validation-failed`, `generation-error`, `dispatch-failed`, `envelope-contract-violation`, and `unclassified-worker-fault` remain the closed worker classes; `outer-envelope-violation` remains coordinator-authored for pre-resolution or malformed outer input as defined by the shared contract.
- The recovery ledger is coordinator-only: it stores at most three distinct normalized diagnosis identities, spends exactly one attempt for each new eligible in-scope identity, and stops before dispatch when the candidate identity is already present. A changed worker failure class does not make a duplicate cause new, while a genuinely different diagnosed cause may use a remaining slot.
- The cause-locus decision is made from coordinator evidence without delegating coordinator verification to a worker. Any out-of-scope claim must name the artifact and concrete point (for example, the Step 2 verification assertion and the Step 3-created wrapper inventory) before the recovery pool can be bypassed.
- The crystallized edge-case identifiers are preserved explicitly below; `E*` headings inside delta specs are local coverage aliases, not a reassignment of the handoff IDs: **Crystallized E1** — clean cancellation remains outside recovery while coordinator-disproved `completed` and `completed` carrying STOP enter diagnosis; **Crystallized E2** — pre-resolution outer-envelope failure remains outside recovery; **Crystallized E3** — worker `failure_class` is a diagnostic hint, not the eligibility gate; **Crystallized E4** — an evidence-backed in-scope worker failure may use one distinct recovery slot; **Crystallized E5** — an evidence-backed out-of-scope cause spends zero and uses hand-back/owner repair; **Crystallized E6** — coordinator rejection remains distinct from worker-authored failure; **Crystallized E7** — continuation/transport loss stops recovery without replacement; **Crystallized E8** — bounded plan repair conserves the Verification Checklist's Coverage Signature; **Crystallized E9** — unrecoverability veto and blocking-contradiction precedence remain authoritative; **Crystallized E10** — the three-slot ledger exhausts only after three distinct diagnoses and stops early on duplicates; **Crystallized E11** — an unclassified worker fault with a coordinator-located in-scope cause may recover; **Crystallized E12** — unpassable RED STOP is failed/blocking-contradiction with concrete evidence; **Crystallized E13** — unpassable GREEN STOP is failed/blocking-contradiction with concrete evidence; **Crystallized E14** — independent coordinator verification remains authoritative without scope or coverage reduction.
