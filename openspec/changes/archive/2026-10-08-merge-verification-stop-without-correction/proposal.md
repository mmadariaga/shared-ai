**Complexity**: medium

## Why

Merge verification previously repeated the full test command after a failed first or second round without requiring an applied correction or evidence that another execution would be useful. Repeating an unchanged failing suite consumes time without improving the resolution.

## What Changes

- Step 8 now chooses between a reviewed applied correction, a recorded concrete repeat reason, and immediate failed verification closure before another execution.
- An applied correction requires tool-confirmed changed bytes in authorized files against the current round's pre-write snapshot and a passed independent review. Proposed changes, reports, no-op writes, and cumulative changed-file lists do not establish this evidence.
- Without either retry justification, the coordinator records `failed-no-correction`, preserves consumed rounds and remaining failures, and continues integration through the existing exhausted-budget navigation without claiming a pass or budget exhaustion.
- Each started execution, including a timeout, consumes one of at most three rounds. Increasing the timeout does not reset the budget. Re-entry retains the current stop's attempts; a new rebase conflict stop receives a fresh budget.
- Lifecycle transitions accept early failed closure for merge collision handling and stopped-rebase finalization.
- Verification notices, the compact finalization summary, and closing output preserve the actual closure reason, consumed rounds, and remaining failures, including unresolved failures from earlier rebase stops.
- Instruction-contract tests cover early closure, current-round correction proof, concrete repeat reasons, timeout accounting, bounded retries, and rebase-stop accounting.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `sai-merge-command`: Make bounded verification retries correction-driven or evidence-backed, with explicit early failed closure.
- `merge-lifecycle-validation-seam`: Accept `failed-no-correction` through the existing failed-verification continuation paths.
- `merge-presentation-seam`: Explain failed verification using the actual closure reason, consumed rounds, and retained failures.
