> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

Real supervised Plan (unattended) runs produced about two overview generations per design, each a 150-400k-token `budget-subagent` run, although Explore already declares its final `Continue` the sole overview-generation pass. The probable early trigger is the `overview` pointer that the opted-in variant of `design-standalone@1` delivers right after `validation`, because the supervised adapter seeded the variant from `overview_language` on its first progress emit.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-plan-unattended.md`: the supervised design adapter always seeds `--with-overview false` on the first progress emit, including on a design-phase retry after a new reset and regardless of `overview_language`, so the `overview` pointer is never delivered during supervised runs. The routing stays coordinator-side; the design worker never touches `sai-state`, and standalone `/sai-2-design` keeps seeding from raw `--overview-lang` presence.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-plan-unattended.md`: the final generation-trigger continuation now carries an explicit instruction naming `@sai/commands/design/steps/overview.md`, because that continuation carries no `Active step:` pointer. It remains the sole generation pass; with `overview_language` `None` nothing is generated.
- `sai/commands/design/phase-contract.md`: § Variant initialization records the supervised route as the one exception to the opt-in seed.
- `test/step-machine-wiring.test.js`: the supervised first-emit assertion now expects `--with-overview false`, and a new test pins the unopted seed, the `regardless of overview_language` wording, the generation trigger naming `steps/overview.md`, and the phase-contract exception.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `supervised-design-overview-generation`: a single overview generation per supervised design phase, at the final generation-trigger continuation.

### Modified Capabilities

- `step-pointer-task-disclosure`: the first combined progress emit of the supervised design route seeds the unopted variant.
- `pipeline-design-phase-chaining`: the chained design terminal's generation trigger names the overview step file and is the sole generation.
- `design-coordinator`: the Continue generation-trigger continuation names the overview step file.

## Impact

- Modified files: `sai/commands/design/phase-contract.md`, `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-plan-unattended.md`, `test/step-machine-wiring.test.js`.
- Standalone `/sai-2-design` generation behavior is unchanged. Supervised runs render no overview during the review rounds; the overview exists only after the design phase closes.
- Confirming the early trigger against a real opencode trace was not performed; the cause is contract-verified only.
Out of scope: design.md, tasks.md, implementation.md — not generated by /sai-backfill
