**Complexity**: high

## Why

Repeated scope, no-suite, and finalization decisions interrupted branch integration even when the intended action was fixed. The former no-suite refusal could also describe code fusion as unperformed after resolution content had already been applied.

## What Changes

- Resolve every affected conflict file in both normal and fast-track modes; remove partial-scope choices and lifecycle branches.
- Preserve normal-mode apply/revise/decline strategy approval. In fast-track, present and validate each complete strategy before continuing the same worker to apply it, including later conflict stops.
- Report missing automated verification as `unavailable` and continue without a question or simulated answer. Preserve the three-round correction budget and report remaining failures.
- Treat command invocation as authorization for local merge finalization, continuation at each resolved rebase stop, and a collision-repair commit after a finished rebase.
- Keep finalization authority invocation-local. Preserve Git checks, remaining safety confirmations, coordinator-owned Git operations, unrelated changes, and failure-state reporting.
- Replace scope and authorization task-panel branches with full-resolution and finalization entries.
- Align shared cards, policies, documentation, and Claude Code/opencode wrapper descriptions.
- Update regression assertions for both strategy modes, unavailable verification, successive rebase stops, collision repair, and preserved safety boundaries.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `sai-merge-command`: full affected-file resolution, mode-specific strategy application, unavailable verification, automatic local finalization, and preserved mutation boundaries.
- `merge-branch-scope-presentation`: unconditional full conflict scope without partial-scope options.
- `merge-presentation-seam`: strategy presentation before mode-specific application, compact finalization information, and updated task-panel semantics.
- `sai-fast-track-flag`: merge method pinning and automatic application after strategy presentation; general unattended behavior remains independent of the flag.
- `merge-batched-questions`: language-only Batch 2 and normal-mode-only strategy approval.
- `rebase-paths`: automatic continuation at resolved stops and automatic final collision-repair commits without an extra clean-rebase commit.
