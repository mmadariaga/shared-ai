> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.
## Why
Commit `4c1dd2b7` moved the path-specific next-step handoff from its pre-selector position to the post-selector `Manual`/unmapped branch, so choosing `Plan - Unattended` or `Direct Build - Unattended` hid the paste-ready `/sai-1-spec` instruction. Restoring the handoff to the shared crystallization-turn close makes the manual handoff visible on every crystallization turn again, while `Manual` stays non-dispatching.
## What Changes
- `sai/commands/explore/instructions.md`: the shared crystallization-turn close now emits the path-specific next-step handoff immediately after the block's `---` separator and before the keep-window recommendation and selector, choosing the single-change, sliced, or inline-refusal wording; the `Manual`/unmapped reference line refers to the already-emitted handoff and forbids re-emitting a recommendation, handoff, or selector.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md`: items 5, 6, and 7 now describe the close as handoff → recommendation → selector; the inline-refusal path emits its copy/start-new-chat handoff once before the selector instead of only after a `Manual` response, and a `Manual`/unmapped response does not re-emit it.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-selector.md`: the selector remains the close's final emission after the handoff and recommendation; the `Manual`/unmapped branch refers to the already-emitted handoff and must not re-emit or restate it; every later selector re-emission emits the handoff once before the selector again; neither the language gate nor `--fast-track` exempts the handoff or the selector.
- `test/explore-pipeline-selector.test.js`: Step 4 assertions now pin the pre-selector placement, the single pre-selector emission of each path wording, the absence of post-selector duplication, and the unchanged localization literals.
- `test/explore-contract-preservation.test.js`: the contract-preservation allowlist accepts the restored pre-selector handoff line as an allowed difference.
## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `explore-crystallization-block`: the shared close emits the path-specific next-step handoff before the recommendation and selector on every crystallization path, and `Manual`/unmapped refers to it once without re-emission.
- `explore-pipeline-selector`: the `Manual`/unmapped branch no longer emits the handoff after the selector response; it refers to the pre-selector handoff, and each re-emission repeats the handoff once before the selector.
- `explore-crystallization-language-gate`: the localized next-step guidance is the pre-selector handoff prose; `/sai-1-spec`, `/sai-2-design`, and `review-loop` stay verbatim English.
