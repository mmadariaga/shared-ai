> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

openspec/schemas/ is shipped product: it is byte-copied by bin/setup.js into every consumer project and enforced by contract tests. Product fixes such as the templates/tasks.md reorder cannot run under today's total openspec/ ban, as seen in required-documentation-relevance-notes.

## What Changes

- sai/commands/explore/direct-build-worker.md now states shipped product schemas under `openspec/schemas/**` are writable, narrows the total `openspec/` ban to an except-schemas ban, and keeps `openspec/specs/**`, `openspec/changes/**`, and `openspec/config.yaml` forbidden or reserved.
- sai/commands/explore/instructions.md now states the schemas-allowed implementer boundary (never under `openspec/` except `openspec/schemas/**`) for the Direct Build exception.
- sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md step-1 detector now lists `openspec/specs/**`, `openspec/changes/**`, and `openspec/config.yaml` as violations with `openspec/schemas/**` permitted and never a violation, and recovery now fires only for non-bookkeeping `openspec/` targets other than `openspec/schemas/**`.
- test/direct-build-schema-writes.test.js (new, 85 lines, 6 tests) now asserts the new boundary, the detector exclusion, the instructions boundary, backfill and archive closed sets with no schemas duplication, and final-commit staging of the implementer union.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `direct-build-schema-scope`: Direct Build implementer schema scope — `openspec/schemas/**` as writable shipped product, contract-test enforcement of the schemas-allowed boundary, and final-commit inclusion of schema files.

### Modified Capabilities

- `auto-fast-implement-worker`: Write containment now permits `openspec/schemas/**` while keeping `openspec/specs/**`, `openspec/changes/**`, and `openspec/config.yaml` forbidden or reserved.
- `explore-pipeline-supervision`: Pre-dispatch compatibility refusal and the Direct Build exception now exclude `openspec/schemas/**` from violations while still flagging `openspec/specs/**`, `openspec/changes/**`, and `openspec/config.yaml`.
