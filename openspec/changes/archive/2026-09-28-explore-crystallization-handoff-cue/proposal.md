## Why

`/sai-explore` can stop after emitting the `Ready to Propose` block instead of continuing to the shared crystallization close and route selector. The new agent-facing cue addresses that handoff boundary without changing the emitted block or the existing close procedure.

## What Changes

- `sai/policies/ready-to-propose-format.md` adds an Explore-only cue after the emitted block template. When `/sai-explore` emits a block, the cue directs same-turn continuation through `Crystallization-turn close (shared, owns B6)` in `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md`, which leads to the route selector.
- The emitted `Ready to Propose` block structure and the existing crystallization-close and route-selection rules remain unchanged.
- `test/explore-pipeline-selector.test.js` verifies that the cue is outside the emitted block, appears once, points to the shared close, and is scoped to `/sai-explore`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `explore-crystallization-handoff`: remind `/sai-explore` to continue the crystallization close immediately after emitting the `Ready to Propose` block.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Modified: `sai/policies/ready-to-propose-format.md` (adds the Explore-only handoff cue outside the emitted block).
- Modified: `test/explore-pipeline-selector.test.js` (checks cue placement, wording, shared-close reference, and Explore-only scope).
- Unchanged: the `Ready to Propose` block structure and emitted content.
- Unchanged: `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md`, including the shared close and its existing completion checks.
- Unchanged: route-selector choices, explicit user selection, and checks that precede route presentation.
- Unchanged: behavior across Claude Code and opencode.
