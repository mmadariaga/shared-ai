## Why

The Direct Build - Unattended terminal report required routine round counts, cap tallies, a full autonomy audit, and verbatim worker summaries. That execution detail could obscure the result and problems that need attention.

## What Changes

- The Direct Build - Unattended terminal close now uses four labeled sections in this order: `Outcome`, `Changes`, `Verification`, and `Incidents`.
- `Outcome` names the archive destination and local commit reference on success, including the short SHA and subject, and states `Nothing was pushed.` It states what completed and what remains when archiving or the commit does not complete.
- `Changes` summarizes material repository changes.
- `Verification` reports failures and limitations, distinguishes pre-existing test failures from failures introduced or worsened by the run, and does not claim the suite passed while failures remain.
- `Incidents` reports run-caused execution problems, contract violations with their violated rule and known effect, and expected generated files excluded from the commit with their reasons.
- Routine rounds without findings, cap tallies, checks without alerts, harmless automatic answers, other execution telemetry, and verbatim worker summaries are omitted from the Direct Build final report.
- The shared autonomy-audit policy records that this final-report exception applies only to Direct Build - Unattended; other audit-emitting routes retain the pinned layout and behavior.
- Tests cover the report order and content, and verify that the Plan - Unattended report remains unchanged.
