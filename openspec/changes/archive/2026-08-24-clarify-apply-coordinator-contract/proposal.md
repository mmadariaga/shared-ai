> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

The Apply prompts ambiguously describe coordinator reads, change-resolution ordering, and the relationship between the durable Step Projection and dispatch-local progress. This can cause small execution models to block legitimate artifact reads, validate `implementation.md` before the change name exists, or mutate the Step Projection from worker progress events.

## What Changes

- Clarifies that coordination and verification reads needed for prerequisites, change resolution, Step Projection, checklist verification, reporting, and gates are allowed.
- Keeps technical reads used to prepare RED or GREEN writes prohibited, along with RED/GREEN verification runs and production or test edits by the coordinator.
- Orders Apply processing as global prerequisites, fast-track parsing, change resolution, implementation-plan validation, and then behavior loading.
- Moves the existing `implementation.md` missing-artifact check after change resolution while preserving its stop literal.
- Defines worker progress as an ephemeral, dispatch-local view tied to an immutable per-dispatch plan.
- Makes the coordinator the sole owner of durable Step Projection rendering, state, and checkbox synchronization.
- Adds contract-level tests for prerequisite ordering and Step Projection ownership.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `apply-coordinator-read-boundary`: Allows bounded coordination and verification reads while preserving the technical-write preparation boundary.
- `apply-change-resolution-ordering`: Requires implementation-plan validation only after the change has been resolved.
- `apply-step-projection-ownership`: Keeps worker progress local to its dispatch and prevents it from mutating the durable Step Projection.
