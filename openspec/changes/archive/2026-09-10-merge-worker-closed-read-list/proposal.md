## Why

A clean conflict-free merge burned approximately 200k tokens in the merge-worker session alone. The full contract stack was re-sent every turn, plus roughly 1000 lines of speculative coordinator, presentation, and spec reads and multi-thousand-line re-deliberation over the language choice and a single-file no-collision result. The change tightens the merge worker so it reads only what its contract names and defaults its summaries to English until a working language exists.

## What Changes

- Modified `sai/commands/merge/worker.md` (24 insertions, 2 deletions): added a `Read discipline (closed read list)` section naming the three permitted fetches, banning proactive reads of other files under `sai/commands/merge/` with `coordinator.md`, `presentation.md`, and `lifecycle.md` named, banning proactive policy spec records while keeping `sai/policies/question-context.md` required via `worker-core`, permitting affected repository content reads only when the active step requires them with no closed path list, defaulting summaries to English until a `working_language` is selected with pinned questions and stop texts verbatim, and prohibiting repetition of a settled read-only check within the same stretch.
- Updated the `Lifecycle` summary clause in the same file: the summary is in English until a `working_language` is selected and in the selected working language thereafter.
- Deferred splitting `merge/instructions.md` (1006 lines): reference risk across worker, coordinator, and presentation references outweighed unmeasured gain for this slice, so the slice keeps read discipline plus language default plus no-reprove only.
