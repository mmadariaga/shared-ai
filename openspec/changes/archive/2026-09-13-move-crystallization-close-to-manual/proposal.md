**Complexity**: low (4 files, crystallization close ordering)

## Why
The pre-selector handoff and recommendation make no sense on delegated `Plan` and `Direct Build` routes and confuse consent; only `Manual` needs paste-ready guidance. The staged change moves both emissions to post-`Manual` so crystallization turns emit only block(s) plus the pipeline selector.

## What Changes
- `sai/commands/explore/instructions.md`: shared close now emits `Ready to Propose` block(s) with `---` plus the selector only before selection; handoff and recommendation definitions moved to post-`Manual`; inline-refusal stays outside the close with its immediate handoff and no selector; selector re-emissions carry no prior handoff.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md`: items 5 and 6 close with block-plus-selector only and no handoff or recommendation before selection; item 7 inline-refusal keeps its immediate handoff without a selector and stays outside the shared close; emission guarantee and same-turn block guard narrowed to items 5 and 6; order identical on both harnesses as block with `---` then a single selector.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-selector.md`: selector is the final emission after the final block with no handoff and no recommendation before selection; `Manual` and unmapped answers emit once the closing path handoff plus the keep-window-open recommendation with no second selector; `Plan - Unattended` and `Direct Build - Unattended` never emit handoff or recommendation; every re-emission carries no prior handoff and every new `Manual` re-emits once; `last_crystallization_set` tracks items 5 and 6 only.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/review-loop.md`: crystallization-close reference narrowed from items 5/6/7 to items 5/6.
- Known limitation left behind: `Manual` readers get one extra message after selection, traded for clean delegated routes with no misleading paste instructions.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- None — all staged behavior modifies existing explore close mechanics.

### Modified Capabilities
- `explore-crystallization-block`: shared close ordering for single and sliced paths, post-`Manual` handoff plus recommendation, inline-refusal outside the close.
- `explore-pipeline-selector`: block-plus-selector final emission, `Manual` post-emission once with no second selector, `Plan` and `Direct Build` no-emission, re-emission without prior handoff.
- `crystallization-close`: block-plus-selector guarantee with no pre-selector handoff or recommendation, same-turn guard, premature-selector correction order.
