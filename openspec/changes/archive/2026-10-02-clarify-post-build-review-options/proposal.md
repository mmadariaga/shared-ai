**Complexity**: low

## Why

The previous successful completion message recommended only `/sai-5-review`, leaving the combined review-and-audits option undisclosed. Users need a clear choice between a general review and a general review with specialized audits selected by the initial assessment, without a promise that all audits will run.

## What Changes

- Updated standalone apply completion guidance to offer `/sai-5-review {name}` for a general review or `/sai-review {name}` to add specialized audits based on the initial assessment performed by `/sai-5-review`.
- Updated build's final apply navigation to use the identical completion message.
- Retained the instruction to use a new chat and the change-name substitution.
- Extended the incomplete-apply instruction to prohibit mentioning either review command while Automated checkboxes remain unmarked.
- Updated focused contract tests to assert identical completion guidance, successful-final-segment conditions, and stopping without automatically chaining review.
- Updated all five overlapping capability requirements that publish the completion literal, retaining their existing scenarios and completion boundaries.
