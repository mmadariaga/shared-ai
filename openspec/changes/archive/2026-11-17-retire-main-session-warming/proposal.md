> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

A retrospective of an opencode `/sai-build` run showed the main session re-arming a 15-minute cache-warming wait for about two hours after the closing report, with no pending work. `sai/policies/cache-warming.md` § Dispose prescribed exactly that: the main session kept arming until its `max` window closed after its closing report. That spends usage allowance on a session that has nothing left to do. This change ends main-session warming when the command finishes, and makes `wait.js` enforce it so a model that forgets the rule on a later wake still arms nothing.

## What Changes

- `skills/universal/sai-cache-warmer/wait.js` gains a `retire --chain <chain>` verb. It writes a `retired_ms` mark into the main session's chain record under the chain lock and prints `{"status"}`: `retired`, `already-retired` (idempotent, keeping the first instant), `absent` (no record), or `refused` (a subagent chain, exit 1; a subagent's chain is ended by its dispatcher's dispose and `close`). Without `--chain` it exits 2.
- On a retired chain, the arm check returns `launch: false`, `warm --chain` and `warm --state` return `finished` without waiting and without taking ownership, and the live wait is not stopped: it finishes its interval and its wake answers `finished` instead of `continue`.
- A main-session `begin` on the same chain rewrites the record without the mark, so the next command invocation in the same chat reactivates the chain.
- `sai/policies/cache-warming.md` gains the terms **Retire** and **Terminal output** and a `## Retire at terminal output` section that single-sources the rule: right before the terminal output of a SAI command (a completed close, a stop, a block, or an unanswered escalation), the main session runs `retire` in the foreground. Only the outermost command's last message retires the chain, not the close of a segment of `/sai-plan`, `/sai-build`, or `/sai-deep-review`. The `/sai-explore` hand-off close and ordinary Explore discussion turns do not retire. A session with no chain retires nothing, and a failed call prints one line and the terminal output goes on. Claude Code and opencode run the same call, with no task handle and no shell removal. § Begin notes that a later `begin` clears the mark, and the § Dispose sentence "keeps arming until `max` minutes have passed" is replaced by the retire.
- The "Terminal output" bullet of `sai/policies/remember.md` points to that section, so coordinator cards do not restate it.
- `docs/project-settings.md` § Cache warming describes the retire at terminal output in place of warming until `max` after the closing report (scope drift: documentation not named in the statement of intent).
- `test/cache-warmer.test.js` covers the verb, its statuses and CLI, the refused re-arm, the live wait finishing its interval, `begin` clearing the mark, and the single-sourced policy text.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None

### Modified Capabilities

- `agent-self-warming`: the main session no longer keeps arming after its closing report; it retires its chain at its command's terminal output, and `wait.js` refuses to re-arm a retired chain until the next main-session `begin`.

## Impact

- Modified: `skills/universal/sai-cache-warmer/wait.js`, `sai/policies/cache-warming.md`, `sai/policies/remember.md`, `docs/project-settings.md`, `test/cache-warmer.test.js`.
- New files: none.
- Behavior: a command invoked in the same chat more than one interval after the previous command ended starts with a cold cache. The `/sai-explore` hand-off wait keeps its fixed-deadline behavior. No user question is added.
- Out of scope: design.md, tasks.md, implementation.md — not generated by /sai-backfill
