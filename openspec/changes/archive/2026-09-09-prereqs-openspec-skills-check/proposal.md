## Why

The OpenSpec prerequisite preflight proved the binary but not the skills — two different artifacts in two different places (binary on PATH, skills in the active harness's project-local skills root). A user with the binary but no skills passed the preflight and died mid-flight when the skill fetch failed, worst inside `/sai-1-spec` where the fetch happens in the already-dispatched worker at the proposal step. The staged diff adds the fourth deterministic precondition check to `sai/tools/prereqs.js` so the preflight predicts whether a future `Fetch @skills/<name>/SKILL.md` will resolve.

## What Changes

- `sai/tools/prereqs.js`: the `check` sub-command now requires `--require-openspec-skills opencode|claude`; a missing or invalid value is a usage error (exit 2), never a halt. After `cli`, `dir`, and `schema` pass, a fourth `skills` check stats `<cwd>/.opencode/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` or `<cwd>/.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` for the three required OpenSpec skills (`openspec-explore`, `openspec-propose`, `openspec-archive-change`) — file stat, not directory; project-local roots only, no `homedir()` or XDG handling; presence-only.
- A missing-skills halt exits 1 with `failed_check: 'skills'`, `reason: openspec-skill-missing`, and a structured `missing_skills: string[]` field aggregating every absent skill and the probed root.
- `sai/policies/prereqs-check.md`: four-checks wording, per-harness byte-identical invocation (`--require-openspec-skills claude` / `opencode`), a fourth `failed_check` → remediation literal mapping, the usage-error rule, and the interactive `openspec init` remedy.
- `sai/commands/explore/body.md` and `sai/commands/meta-review/coordinator.md`: consumer cards migrated to the flagged invocation and the four-checks prerequisite list.
- `AGENTS.md`: the "Prerequisite check" section now documents four checks, the flag contract, the aggregated halt, and the exempt-command boundary (`sai-commit`, `sai-merge`, `/sai-worktree`, `/sai-retire-docs` gain no skills check).
- `test/prereqs-tool.test.js`: coverage for the mandatory-flag usage error, the aggregated missing-skills result, directory-without-`SKILL.md` (E1), other-harness copies not satisfying the check (E3), the aggregated halt payload after the first three checks pass, the all-present pass, and the policy literal updates.
