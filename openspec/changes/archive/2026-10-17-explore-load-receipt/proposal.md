> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

A user message that arrives while `/sai-explore` is still loading can take over the session, so exploration runs without its contract and nothing detects the unfinished load (observed in opencode with a queued `/from-backlog`). The load had no checkable end: `sai-state spawn` ran only at the first stage event, so no tool output marked the load as complete.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/explore/body.md` gains a `## Load receipt` section, the single source of the receipt rule. The load receipt is the `verdict: pass` of the `prereqs.js` check plus the id returned by `sai-state spawn`, both produced after the most recent `/sai-explore` invocation in the chat. The section defines the per-turn receipt check with a recovery load, deferred-message handling in arrival order (including deferred skill or command invocations), cancellation during the load, the prerequisite halt, spawn failure, and the shared behavior for Claude Code and opencode.
- `sai/commands/explore/body.md` runs `sai-state spawn --key <stable-key>` as the final step of the load, after the instruction loads and before `## Run`, and retains the returned id as the receipt's session id.
- `sai/commands/explore/instructions.md` § Stage machines replaces the per-turn spawn-then-emit cycle: every stage-event turn emits against the owning machine on the receipt id from the load and opens no session of its own.
- `commands/claude/sai-explore.md` and `commands/opencode/sai-explore.md` each gain one `Receipt:` context-pointer line, first after the frontmatter, pointing to `body.md` § Load receipt; the wrappers carry no rule text.
- Contract tests pin the shape: `test/explore-contract-preservation.test.js` asserts the wrapper pointer, the `## Load receipt` section and its rules, spawn as the final load step, and the receipt-id emit; `test/explore-stage-machine-consumption.test.js` asserts the receipt-id emit in place of the spawn-then-emit cycle; `test/install-claude.test.js` and `test/install-opencode.test.js` assert the pointer in the installed wrappers; `test/claude-default-preset-alignment.test.js` refreshes the opencode projection fingerprint.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `explore-load-receipt`: the `/sai-explore` load receipt, the per-turn receipt check with recovery load, deferred-message handling during the load, cancellation, prerequisite halt, spawn failure, and the wrapper context pointer.

### Modified Capabilities

- `explore-stage-machine`: `sai-state spawn` runs once as the final step of the explore load, and stage events emit on the receipt id instead of running a spawn-then-emit cycle per turn.
- `command-wrapper-body`: the `sai-explore` wrapper body admits one `Receipt:` context-pointer line, first after the frontmatter, as part of the `sai-explore` exception; every other wrapper body is unchanged.
- `thin-wrappers`: the `sai-explore` receipt pointer line becomes a permitted per-file variance of the wrapper template.
- `explore-pre-crystallization-stages`: the sidecar session lifecycle spawns once at the end of the explore load and every stage-event turn emits on the receipt id instead of running spawn then `/emit` per turn.
- `explore-instruction-delivery`: the startup and deferred byte figures are updated to the measured sizes of the explore instruction files.

An interruption before the wrapper pointer is read, combined with a stale cached wrapper that lacks the pointer, stays uncovered until `body.md` loads. Interrupted loads of other commands are not covered.
