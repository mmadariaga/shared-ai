> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

A worker's `unrecoverable: true` veto in `/sai-4-apply` blocked recovery even after explicit user authorization, while coordinator evidence could already declare a veto false. The cheapest model's judgment therefore outranked the human who could see the same evidence. GREEN's stated reason for its test-file prohibition was also partly outdated: test-infrastructure causes already route to the Step's RED owner on their own.

## What Changes

- `recovery-ledger@1` (`sai-state/machines/recovery-ledger.js`) tracks worker vetoes.
  - A coordinator-sent `{kind: veto, step}` event records the active Step's veto and spends nothing.
  - An explicitly authorized `{kind: authorized-veto-override, step, authorized: true}` event lifts exactly that one active veto, appends the Step to `veto_lifts`, and changes neither budgets, key ledgers, nor attempt history.
  - The override is rejected (`no active veto`, `Step is not active`, `unidentified Step`, `explicit authorization required`) with no state change.
  - A first entry into a Step clears any active veto.
- `bin/sai-state.js`: the closed `OBSERVABILITY_FIELDS` set gains `veto` and `veto_override`. The machine's `veto: recorded` and `veto_override: granted` outcomes now reach the emit response, the persisted merged wire, and the stored last outcome, so the coordinator can read them.
- `sai/policies/bounded-recovery.md`: the eligibility condition on the worker veto now also passes when the veto was lifted by an accepted `authorized-veto-override` (the user's explicit authorization, one veto per event, ledger-recorded, changing neither budgets nor history). The `authorized-step-retry` rule is unchanged.
- New disclosed branch file `sai/commands/apply/steps/veto-override.md`. In standalone `/sai-4-apply` and the apply segment of `/sai-build` it:
  - records the veto;
  - shows the worker's evidence, role, blocked Step, affected path, and both budget tallies;
  - asks through the native picker: `Lift the veto` or `I will correct it manually`. The lift is never auto-selected under `--fast-track` or `session_commit_authorized`;
  - sends the override event;
  - continues the same worker with `continue_after_recovery` only for an eligible diagnosis.
- `sai/commands/apply/coordinator.md`:
  - Human override gains the one-line pointer `Worker unrecoverable: true → Fetch @sai/commands/apply/steps/veto-override.md`.
  - The exhausted-Step choice and the blocked-override list now refer to an "un-lifted" veto.
- `sai/commands/apply/green-worker.md`: the prohibition reason now reads as ownership. GREEN's write surface is the Step's production files, test files belong to the Step's RED owner, and when passing needs a test change GREEN returns `blocking-contradiction` with the evidence. The absolute prohibition sentence is kept.
- `test/recovery-ledger-machine.test.js`:
  - three machine-level tests cover veto recording, override rejections, and the single-veto lift;
  - one CLI-level test checks that a `sai-state` emit carries the `veto` record and `veto_override` outcomes on the wire.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `bounded-worker-recovery`: veto eligibility gains a user-authorized, ledger-recorded override. The recovery ledger tracks vetoes and their lifts, and its closed wire field set gains `veto` and `veto_override`.
- `apply-coordinator-ownership`: the coordinator owns the worker veto hand-back with an explicit lift-or-manual choice.
- `apply-red-green-worker-model`: GREEN's test-file prohibition reason is restated as RED ownership with a `blocking-contradiction` hand-back.

## Impact

- New: `sai/commands/apply/steps/veto-override.md`
- Modified: `bin/sai-state.js`, `sai-state/machines/recovery-ledger.js`, `sai/policies/bounded-recovery.md`, `sai/commands/apply/coordinator.md`, `sai/commands/apply/green-worker.md`, `test/recovery-ledger-machine.test.js`

Assertion-weakening, contract-redefinition, and safe-operations stops, `apply-step.js verify`, and the commit gates are unchanged. GREEN's absolute test-file prohibition and its routing are unchanged.
