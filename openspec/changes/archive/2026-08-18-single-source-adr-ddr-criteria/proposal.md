## Why

The three ADR/DDR criteria and the ordered routing test were written three times — in `sai/commands/spec/instructions.md`, `sai/commands/design/instructions.md`, and `sai/commands/implement/instructions.md`. Three copies of one rule meant any correction had to land in three places or the surfaces would drift apart.

The `sai-1` copy was additionally dead. Its own text scoped it to `design.md` ("When `design.md` is being authored"), and the Complexity Derivation Rubric immediately below it said so explicitly — yet it sat inside `/sai-1-spec`, a phase that authors no design decisions. The three copies were not three rules but one rule with three roles: evaluate (`sai-1`, spurious), record the family (`sai-2`), act on it (`sai-3`). Only the middle two are real roles.

- Adds `sai/policies/adr-ddr-criteria.md`, a new shared instruction carrying the three criteria, the ordered routing test, and the never-leave-the-choice-open rule. It is flat rather than parameterized: it declares that it takes no parameters and that the fetch site supplies nothing.
- Deletes the `## ADR/DDR Proposal Check` section from `sai/commands/spec/instructions.md`, and rewrites the cross-reference that deletion orphaned in the Complexity Derivation Rubric, which had read "It is unrelated to the `## ADR/DDR Proposal Check` above, which is scoped to `design.md`."
- Replaces the inline criteria and routing-test text in `sai/commands/design/instructions.md`'s `## Decisions` bullet with `Fetch @sai/policies/adr-ddr-criteria.md`. The `**Record family**: adr|ddr` emission and its byte-exact-label sentence are preserved. The Provenance bullet's back-reference is adjusted from "the three ADR/DDR criteria above" to "the three ADR/DDR criteria" because the criteria are no longer above it.

The change is instruction-surface only: `sai-3` already read the `**Record family**` marker rather than re-deciding the family, so no runtime behavior changed. The `**Provenance**` marker was out of scope and is untouched apart from the one back-reference word.
