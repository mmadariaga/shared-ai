## Why

The staged implementation removes duplicated Auto-fast mutation logic from the dedicated hands worker. Existing backfill and archive workers now retain ownership of mutations belonging to their phases while preserving coordinator validation and authorization.

## What Changes

- The backfill worker adds a read-only preparation route followed by one explicit, coordinator-authorized execution continuation for validated draft artifacts.
- The archive worker adds a read-only preparation route followed by ordered sync, verification, archive move, owned staging, and one authorized local commit.
- Auto-fast selection routes through the existing implementer, backfill, and archive workers.
- The dedicated `autofast-hands-worker` contract, matrix role, bindings, agents, and harness projections are removed.
- Ordinary backfill and archive behavior remains coordinator-write-owned.
- Scope-drift behavior: Auto-fast ADR/DDR handling suppresses decision-record drafts.
- The staged implementation retains path-scoped staging before backfill preparation; this is not treated as a global no-mutation-before-execute guarantee.
