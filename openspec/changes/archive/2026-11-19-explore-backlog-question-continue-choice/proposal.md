> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

The round 1 backlog question of `/sai-explore` offered only `Yes` and `No`, so a user who only wanted to report an issue had to interrupt the flow with free text to skip the branch and route steps, as happened when #474 was reported. The question now captures in one answer both whether to publish to the backlog and whether to continue to implementation.

## What Changes

- The round 1 backlog question in `sai/commands/explore/steps/route-selector.md` keeps its question text and the word `backlog`, and offers three options in this order with stable values: `Publish and continue with implementation` (`publish-continue`), `Publish and stop` (`publish-stop`), and `Do not publish and continue with implementation` (`skip-continue`), with the Spanish labels `Publicar y continuar con la implementación`, `Publicar y parar`, and `No publicar y continuar con la implementación`. The **Round rules** paragraph lists the backlog question among the questions with their own options, and keeps Yes/No for the remaining questions.
- `publish-continue` behaves as the former Yes and `skip-continue` as the former No, so the round 1, round 2, and round 3 flow is unchanged for the continue answers.
- The branch question and the assignment question are conditional questions under `sai/policies/question-rounds.md`, whose condition is that the backlog answer is `publish-continue` or `skip-continue`. Under `publish-stop` their answers are recorded as not applicable: no branch is created, nothing is assigned, and round 2 asks only the publication approval, never the branch base. The assignment question's intent form applies only when the backlog answer is `publish-continue`.
- A new `## Publish and stop close` section in `route-selector.md` owns the end of the invocation after `to-backlog` finishes under `publish-stop`: it disposes the live explorers, retires the main-session cache-warming chain, and prints a terminal output that states the actual outcome (the published issue link, or the parent and every child for a sliced set; that no backlog item was published after a cancelled approval or a skip after missing skill access; or the result `to-backlog` reported after its recovery ended without complete success). The close emits no `explore-slice@1` intent, runs no `sai-state close`, never presents round 3, and a later message in the same chat presents no route picker. The `publish-stop` answer lives in conversation state only and is never stored in `explore-slice@1`. An `edit` answer to the publication approval keeps the `publish-stop` decision.
- The **Completion gate** paragraph names the `publish-stop` close as the one path that ends without presenting the route picker.
- The **Free-text answers** paragraph maps a free-text answer to the backlog question onto one stable value by intent: a bare affirmative maps to `publish-continue`, a request to only report or only publish maps to `publish-stop`, a plain decline maps to `skip-continue`, and an ambiguous answer is clarified while the question stays pending.
- `docs/commands/sai-explore.md` describes the three options, the conditional branch and assignment questions, the free-text mapping, and the `Publish and stop` close, and replaces the statements that the round questions are always asked and that the route choice always follows.
- `test/explore-question-rounds.test.js`, `test/explore-step-reachability.test.js`, and `test/explore-pipeline-selector.test.js` assert the three labels, their order and stable values, the Spanish labels, the conditional branch and assignment questions, the `publish-stop` close, and the free-text mapping.

## Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `explore-route-close`: the branch and assignment questions are conditional on a continue answer, round 2 under `publish-stop` asks only the publication approval, and the `publish-stop` close ends the invocation after the publication without round 3.
