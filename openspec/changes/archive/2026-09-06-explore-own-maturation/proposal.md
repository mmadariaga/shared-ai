## Why

The same open discovery questions lived in both sai-explore and sai-1-spec, so answers given in explore were re-asked in spec. Making explore the single owner of discovery questions, trade-offs, WHY, lightweight edge probing, and terminology agreement removes that duplication while keeping explore read-only.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md` — adds four conversation-only rules: Explore-change discovery ownership with a direct ask to move to Review edge cases (a declining answer stays in Explore change, later advancement uses the existing next-step path, never auto-advances on explore's own solid judgment), pre-edge-case terminology agreement travelling as the block Terms with no GLOSSARY.md writes, a pre-step lightweight probe of up to 2 what-if scenarios that does not replace the formal list, and rationale ownership recording agreed trade-offs in Decisions & Rationale plus Trade-offs Accepted and the agreed WHY in Why plus Key constraints.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-language-gates.md` — adds `**Terms**` to the English scaffolding label inventory so the translated-surface rule covers the new block section.
- `sai/policies/ready-to-propose-format.md` — adds the `**Terms**` block section (agreed term: definition, or None), places it immediately after Key constraints and before Edge Cases, and adds the Terms field rule (conversation-only agreement, never a file write).
- Preserved: explore performs no file writes and grants no new write scope; block validation in spec stays by concepts, not byte-exact format.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `discovery-questions`: explore owns open discovery in Explore change, with a direct ask to move to edge cases once the idea is solid and a next-step fallback when declined.
- `lightweight-probe`: systematic pre-review probe of up to 2 what-if scenarios in conversation before the formal edge-case list.
- `rationale-ownership`: trade-offs and WHY owned by explore via the block rationale fields.
- `terminology-agreement`: ambiguous terms fixed before edge cases in conversation, travelling to spec as agreed Terms while explore stays read-only.

### Modified Capabilities

- None — all behavior is new delineation under the four capabilities above; existing specs overlap (see conflict report in run summary) but no verbatim requirement heading is modified.
