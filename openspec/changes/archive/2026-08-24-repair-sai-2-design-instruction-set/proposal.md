**Complexity**: medium

## Why
An audit found a materialized binding instruction that contradicted the opt-in overview-language contract, a feedback gate that referenced a retired interfaces snapshot, orphaned installed design instructions, and paragraph-scale rules that caused drift in small coordinator models. These contradictions and duplicate contract surfaces made `/sai-2-design` behavior unpredictable for budget-tier models.

## What Changes
- Repoints the existing design-related capability contracts to the routed coordinator, worker, and step-owned instruction surfaces.
- Makes valid `--overview-lang` the sole opt-in for overview generation and removes implicit English synthesis when the flag is absent.
- Retires the deleted monolithic design instruction and invocation sources with hash-pinned lifecycle entries for both harnesses.
- Consolidates plan-selection, review-evidence, feedback-gate, and Explore supervision rules onto canonical surfaces.
- Restructures paragraph-scale rules into tables and single-purpose bullets.
- Adds the no-findings design-review branch, restores ten corrupted em dashes, and adds projection and mojibake regression guards.
- Also updates staged command inventories, target-state manifest folding, lifecycle payload documentation, and an unrelated implement-routing regression assertion evidenced by the selected diff.
