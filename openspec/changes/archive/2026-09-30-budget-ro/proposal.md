> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

The full `budget` skill loads the explorer, executor, and task-subagent bindings plus their policies (about 7 calls, about 10 KB), but the spec, review, security, performance, and accessibility phases only ever delegate to `budget-explorer` (`sai/commands/spec/steps/common.md:7`). The language contract also reached those sessions only by accident, through the trigger words of `token-efficient-languages`, while `sai/policies/remember.md` restated the same three rules inline.

Known limitations accepted: none recorded. Non-goal: design and implement keep the full budget skill (design uses `budget-subagent` for the overview); a reduced budget skill for design is decided with its own phase.

## What Changes

- New `skills/universal/budget-ro/SKILL.md` ("budget read-only"), harness-neutral, `compatibility: opencode, claude`. It loads exactly `@skills/budget-explorer/SKILL.md` then `@skills/token-efficient-languages/SKILL.md`, never the executor or task-subagent bindings, and has its own trigger phrases (`budget read-only`, `budget ro`, `read-only budget mode`) instead of the `budget` cost-mode phrases.
- `sai/commands/{spec,review,security,performance,accessibility}/steps/common.md` fetch `@skills/budget-ro/SKILL.md` instead of `@skills/budget/SKILL.md`. Design and implement `steps/common.md` keep the full budget skill.
- `sai/policies/remember.md` § Language is one `Fetch @skills/token-efficient-languages/SKILL.md` line plus the sentence that the user can override any of the three rules explicitly; the three inline bullets are removed.
- `test/worker-delegation-tools.test.js`: `BUDGET_FETCH` also matches `@skills/budget-ro/SKILL.md`, so a budget-ro Fetch line counts as a delegating load.
- `AGENTS.md`: a table row for `skills/universal/budget-ro/SKILL.md` and the Cost Discipline sentence naming which cards fetch which skill.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `language-contract-single-source`: `remember.md` § Language carries no inline copy of the language rules and fetches `token-efficient-languages` once, keeping the explicit user override.

### Modified Capabilities

- `budget-universal-loader`: adds the `budget-ro` skill requirement; the `budget` skill keeps its three bindings, and its rationale for omitting `token-efficient-languages` now points at `remember.md` fetching it.
- `spec-propose-cost-discipline`: the spec phase's `steps/common.md` fetches `@skills/budget-ro/SKILL.md`.
- `managed-worker-registry`: a `Fetch` line for `@skills/budget-ro/SKILL.md` makes a worker delegating, alongside the four existing budget skills.
