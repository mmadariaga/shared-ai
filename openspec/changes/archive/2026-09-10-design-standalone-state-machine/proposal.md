**Complexity**: medium (4 files, 1 new capability, no breaking change)

## Why
Standalone sai-2-design preloaded its full instruction surface up front, which confuses small and cheap models. Relocating happy-path step routing into a stateful sidecar machine keeps boot minimal while the worker only ever sees its current step. This mirrors the sibling spec-standalone-state-machine mechanism over the design seven-step table.

## What Changes
- Add stateful sidecar machine `sai-state/machines/design-standalone.js` owning standalone happy-path routing with opted-in seven-step and unopted six-step variants.
- Register `design-standalone@1` in `sai-state/registry.js` alongside the three existing machines.
- Update `sai/commands/design/coordinator.md` so standalone runs consult the machine per progress event and wrap its `next.follow` in the unchanged two-line continuation.
- Add `test/design-standalone-machine.test.js` covering registration, happy-path walks, silent ignore, variant immutability, replacement re-resolution, parked behavior, and purity.

## Capabilities
Standalone routing moves out of the coordinator preload into the sidecar; the worker reports progress and follows the delivered pointer.

### New Capabilities
- `design-step-routing`: sidecar machine owning the seven-step happy-path progression (six when unopted) for standalone runs.

### Modified Capabilities
- `reactive-instruction-loading`: per-state follow files plus event-gated contracts for the design steps with no heavy upfront preload.

## Impact
- New: `sai-state/machines/design-standalone.js` — machine table, transition, project, variant handling.
- Modified: `sai-state/registry.js` — one-line registration of `design-standalone@1`.
- Modified: `sai/commands/design/coordinator.md` — standalone sidecar routing paragraph and machine-sourced pointer derivation.
- New: `test/design-standalone-machine.test.js` — happy-path, ignore, variant, replacement, parked, and purity coverage.
