> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

`/sai-3-implement-new` implemented only the tasks of `tasks.md`. When a change directory already held audit reports (`review.md`, `security.md`, `performance.md`, `accessibility.md`), their findings stayed unapplied, so an unattended build left known defects in place. The command now applies the open findings of those reports when the report artifacts exist, through the same step cycle it uses for tasks.

## What Changes

- After every task of `tasks.md` is checked, the `/sai-3-implement-new` coordinator applies the open fixable findings of the audit reports that exist in the change directory, as defined by `sai/policies/finding-state.md`, which the coordinator now fetches.
- Each finding, or a few findings that share a location and a fix, runs as one step of the existing RED, GREEN, review, and commit cycle. RED runs only when the finding concerns behavior a test can pin and no test pins yet; otherwise the step goes to GREEN directly. Either path is the plan, not a deviation.
- The step's commit carries the fix together with the ` (FIXED)` mark the coordinator appends to the finding's heading line. Fixed findings, like checked tasks, are never done again. Questions stay open for the user; a finding judged mistaken, or whose fix contradicts the plan, stays open with a deviation; a fix that would change `proposal.md`'s What or Why is force majeure.
- The no-work start path now requires both that every task is checked and that no audit report holds an open fixable finding.
- The coordinator reads the existing audit reports at start, writes only the fixed marks in them, and resumes from the fixed marks as well as from `git log` and the `tasks.md` checkboxes.
- The run closes as completed only when every open fixable finding is fixed or carries a deviation; `build-log.md` and the closing report list the audit findings fixed, the open Questions, and the findings left open with their reasons.
- RED and GREEN briefs may carry audit findings in place of plan references. For a finding routed to RED, RED writes one test that asserts the corrected behavior, fails by assertion on the current code, and names the finding's identifier. Workers leave the audit reports and the fixed marks to the coordinator.
- `sai/policies/finding-state.md` names `/sai-3-implement-new` as a consumer of open findings and as a writer of fixed marks, which it adds in the commit of the step that fixed each finding.
- `AGENTS.md` and `docs/commands/sai-3-implement-new.md` describe the audit-report pass, and `test/implement-new-family.test.js` pins the new coordinator, worker, and RED-step text.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `implement-new-loop`: loads `finding-state`, applies open fixable audit findings after the tasks through the step cycle, marks them fixed in the step commit, and extends the no-work start, coordinator authority, and completion rules.
- `implement-new-run-log`: the completed close condition and the `build-log.md` contents cover the audit findings.
- `implement-new-workers`: briefs may carry audit findings; RED writes one failing test per finding; workers leave audit reports and fixed marks to the coordinator.
- `implement-new-resilience`: resumption and store-loss rebuild read the fixed marks and continue at the first open fixable finding once every task is checked.
- `finding-state`: `/sai-3-implement-new` joins the consumers that act on open findings only and writes the fixed mark in the commit of the step that fixed the finding.
- `audit-artifact-ingestion`: the Apply/Discard judgment is scoped to `/sai-3-implement`; `/sai-3-implement-new` disposes of findings through its own contract.
- `audit-finding-escalation`: the Apply/Discard/Escalate classification is scoped to `/sai-3-implement`; `/sai-3-implement-new` disposes of findings through its own contract.
