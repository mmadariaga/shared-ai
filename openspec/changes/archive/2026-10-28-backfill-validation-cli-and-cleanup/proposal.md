> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

`sai/tools/validate-backfill-drafts.js` reported a missing OpenSpec CLI with the same generic failure as invalid drafts, so receivers returned it to the backfill worker as a draft correction and spent attempts that editing drafts can never recover. The accepted closures of `/sai-backfill` and Direct Build named only the transfer directory, although `sai/policies/backfill-draft-validation.md` already required removing the temporary validation projects, and the backfill `draft` obstacle definition did not name a rejection by the OpenSpec CLI validation.

## What Changes

- `sai/tools/validate-backfill-drafts.js` probes the OpenSpec CLI before collecting drafts; when the CLI cannot be launched it returns `ok: false` with the distinct `reason: openspec-cli-unavailable` and its diagnostic, creates no temporary project, and that reason wins even when the drafts are also invalid. A CLI rejection carries no such reason.
- `sai/policies/backfill-draft-validation.md` defines `openspec-cli-unavailable` as force majeure: the receiver escalates at once, requests no new draft, spends no `draft` attempt, writes nothing, and keeps the evidence. Rejections, incomplete output, failed copies, and changed inputs still return to the same worker for correction. The cleanup rule now names each returned `project_dir` and `temp-root.js remove --harness <claude|opencode> --dir <project_dir>`, and keeps the projects at a failed or escalated closure.
- `sai/commands/backfill/coordinator.md` escalates `openspec-cli-unavailable` as force majeure with an option to install or restore the CLI and re-run validation, adds a rejection by the OpenSpec CLI validation to the `draft` obstacle definition, and removes the temporary validation projects at an accepted closure next to the transfer directory.
- `sai/commands/explore/direct-build-coordinator.md` applies the same force-majeure exception in spec review (no `step-4` attempt) and removes the temporary validation projects retained in run state at accepted route closure, keeping them at a failed or escalated closure.
- `test/validate-backfill-drafts.test.js` covers the distinct reason, its precedence over invalid drafts, its absence on a CLI rejection, and the card and policy text of both receivers.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `pre-write-backfill-validation`: an unavailable OpenSpec CLI returns a distinct reason handled as force majeure, and temporary validation projects are removed only at accepted closure.
- `routed-backfill-command`: the `draft` obstacle includes a rejection by the OpenSpec CLI validation, and the CLI-unavailable reason escalates as force majeure without a `draft` attempt.
