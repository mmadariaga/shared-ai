## Why

Readers who join a public chat after work has moved between contexts can encounter unexplained internal terms and references. The implemented policy adds simple wording, brief explanations, and enough local context to make user-facing messages easier to understand and act on.

## What Changes

- Adds `sai/policies/public-chat.md` as the shared policy for explanatory text visible in public chat.
- Loads that policy from the shared command-runner and worker-core protocol layers.
- Covers coordinator text, utility-command text, and worker-authored `question`, `message`, and `summary` fields presented by coordinators.
- Preserves fixed protocol text, machine-readable content, identifiers, picker options, technical formats, code, and persistent artifacts.
- Updates `question-context.md` to reference the broader policy while remaining specialized for decision prompts.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `public-chat-communication-policy`: Defines plain wording, context carry-over, concise explanations of non-obvious references, and protocol-preserving coverage for visible explanatory chat text.

### Modified Capabilities

- `question-context-policy`: Retains the specialized decision-prompt anatomy while delegating broader public-chat readability and context rules to the shared policy.
