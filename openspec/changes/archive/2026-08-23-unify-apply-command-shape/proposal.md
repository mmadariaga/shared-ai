> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

**Complexity**: medium (behavior-preserving relocation; 3 modified capabilities; no breaking change)

## Why

`/sai-4-apply` answered shared questions differently from every other routed phase: the runner loop contract was fetched twice (once by the coordinator card, once by `invocation.md`), and substantive behaviors — the session-scoped commit authorization flag, the fast-track branch auto-stay rule, and the chained-activation carve-out — lived on the shell card instead of on surfaces that BOTH entry paths (the standalone wrapper boot and the `/sai-build` chained segment) load. Every contributor had to learn a second mental model just for apply. The motivation was comprehension cost, not behavior: this change reduces the perceptible exceptions from roughly ten to the three irreducible ones, at zero behavior change.

Known limitations and deliberate deferrals accepted with this change:
- Comprehension-only gain — no observable behavior improvement for end users of `/sai-4-apply`.
- Relocation churn across manifest checks and tests landed in this single change rather than staged across several.
- Cross-phase failure-recovery unification remains future work: apply keeps its own Known-False Report Recovery (same-worker ledger) while sai-1/2/3 keep replacement reconstruction — deliberately out of scope because it would touch five commands.
- A steps/ instruction library for RED/GREEN workers and a fixed canonical progress_plan for apply were considered and rejected for now: dispatch sessions are short and both phases always run, so just-in-time delivery buys nothing; the dynamic Step projection stays the only visible list.
- The three irreducible apply differences remain as documented exceptions by design, not debt to eliminate.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/apply/coordinator.md` gains the relocated sections verbatim: `## Session-scoped commit authorization flag`, `## Fast-track branch auto-stay`, and `## Chained activation (composition path)` — surfaces loaded by both entry paths. It now owns the single fetch of `runner.md`.
- `sai/commands/apply/invocation.md` becomes shell-thin: it retains prerequisite checks, fast-track parse (sole authority), change resolution, behavior loads (`sai-learnings-format.md`, `remember.md`), and the `## Completion` section; it loses the three relocated sections and its `runner.md` fetch line.
- AGENTS.md documents the three irreducible apply differences as deliberate design: (1) dynamic Step projection from `implementation.md` instead of a static `progress_plan`, (2) fresh blind workers per Step, (3) coordinator-owned git operations at the two commit-authorization gates.
- Guard tests updated: the fast-track pre-activation assertion retargets to the coordinator card; new guards pin single-owner `runner.md` fetching, flag/auto-stay ownership on the coordinator card, and fast-track parse sole authority in `invocation.md`.
- Main specs that pinned the old behavior homes are updated via delta specs: apply-routed-card-set, commit-auth-gate, routed-commit-command.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None — the change is behavior-preserving relocation plus documentation.

### Modified Capabilities

- **apply-routed-card-set** — loading contract moves the `runner.md` loop-contract fetch to the coordinator card alone; fast-track gate contracts move to the coordinator card; the three irreducible apply differences gain documented normative status.
- **commit-auth-gate** — the apply-side `session_commit_authorized` lifecycle definition is owned by the apply coordinator card instead of the invocation shell.
- **routed-commit-command** — the single-source citation of `commit-rules.md ## Authorization Scope` moves from the apply invocation to the apply coordinator card.
