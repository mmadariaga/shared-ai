> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

Clean-session enforcement was duplicated: every routed coordinator card opened with a six-line `# Isolation Mode` block, and several cards re-fetched `@sai/orchestration/command-runner.md` even though the harness boot adapter had already loaded it before card selection. Nine copies of the same safety block invited drift between phases and between harnesses, and the redundant fetches made the boot adapter's pre-card load ambiguous. Moving the guard to a single preamble line in each boot adapter removes the duplication while preserving the guarantee.

## What Changes

- Both harness boot adapters (`sai/adapters/claude/boot.md`, `sai/adapters/opencode/boot.md`) gain one byte-identical clean-session preamble line — "This invocation starts clean: disregard prior conversational context except where a fetched contract explicitly directs otherwise." — inserted immediately before the `@sai/orchestration/command-runner.md` fetch.
- All nine routed coordinator cards (`sai/commands/{spec,design,implement,review,security,performance,accessibility,apply,build}/coordinator.md`) lose their per-card `# Isolation Mode` block, their redundant `command-runner.md` re-fetch, and their `worker-core.md` fetch (worker-authored lifecycle content belongs to workers); `spec/coordinator.md` also drops its inline "Preserve Isolation Mode." sentence. MANDATORY STOP literals stay byte-for-byte.
- `sai/commands/apply/runner.md`: the ordered nine-field report extension moves into `## Dispatch-Kind Report Table`.
- `sai/commands/apply/invocation.md`: loses its isolation block; the `session_commit_authorized` reset rationale now cites the session-start boot preamble.
- Eight routed launchers (`{spec,design,implement,build,review,security,performance,accessibility}/launcher.md`) drop the duplicated coordinator-card fetch line; behaviour skills and worker bindings stay. Card selection is owned by the boot adapter alone.
- Tests and `fixtures/thin-command-wrappers-baseline.json` flip to lock the new invariants: coordinators doNotMatch runner/worker-core fetches, launchers carry no coordinator-card fetch, boots carry the preamble.
- `AGENTS.md` and `README.md` document the relocated convention.
