## Why

The crystallization close repeated its sequence across the single-change and sliced paths. A final `---` could end the turn before the ordered inventory was recorded and the route selector was presented. The staged change makes the shared close explicit and preserves its failure boundaries.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md` makes the final separator start one shared close, defines its ordered completion conditions, and makes the single-change and sliced paths invoke it once.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/route-selector.md` consumes only a successful inventory event whose `next.follow` points to the selector.
- `test/explore-pipeline-selector.test.js` verifies ordered close conditions, single- and multi-change inventory recording without reset, and panel, store, and step-load failure boundaries.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- **crystallization-close** — Completes crystallization in one turn through an ordered shared close covering the final block, stage panel, inventory recording, and route selector.
- **explore-crystallization-block** — Makes the protocol step the shared-close source while preserving inline-refusal, handoff, recommendation, and route-choice behavior.
- **explore-pre-crystallization-stages** — Moves stage-TODO clearing to the shared close after final block emission while preserving the existing phase-A/phase-B panel lifecycle.

## Impact

- The Explore crystallization instructions now have one executable close sequence for single-change and sliced emissions.
- Route-selector presentation is gated on successful ordered inventory recording and the returned selector pointer.
- Stage-TODO clearing occurs in the shared close rather than being treated as complete at first slice identification.
- Existing panel lifecycle, route options, fast-track gating, inline refusal, and `/sai-1-spec` handoff behavior remain unchanged except for the documented close timing and unavailable-panel distinction.
- The prepared delta set covers `crystallization-close`, `explore-crystallization-block`, and `explore-pre-crystallization-stages`.
- Out of scope: design.md, tasks.md, implementation.md — not generated by /sai-backfill
