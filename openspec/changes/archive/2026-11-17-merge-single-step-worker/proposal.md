> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

Every observed `/sai-merge` conflict stop paid two worker round trips (a `strategy` step, then an `apply` step) although no strategy escalated: a single-region stop took 8.5 minutes of strategy plus 3.5 minutes of apply, and a 49-commit rebase recomputed the same fingerprint procedure in 11 of its 13 stops. The coordinator still sent `strategy` and then `apply` for every stop that needed judgment.

## What Changes

- The merge worker's `strategy` and `apply` steps are merged into one `resolve` step (`sai/commands/merge/steps/resolve.md`, renamed from `steps/apply.md`): the worker decides every conflict by commit intent and writes the resolution through `merge.js write` in the same step, and its completed report carries the `decisions` the coordinator writes to `stop-{n}-confirmed.json` before `merge.js resolution --phase authored`.
- `steps/strategy.md` stays as the strategy-only step for a report that carries `escalations`: nothing is written, the coordinator escalates through the native picker, and then sends `resolve` again with the user's decision.
- `merge.js conflicts` gives every conflicted file a conflict `signature` (path, category, region count, per-region ours and theirs line counts, and an `id` that is the sha256 of `[path, category, region_lines]`), in the stop record and in its stdout summary; region text is not part of it.
- A completed `resolve` report may carry `recipe`, a list of `{path, signature}`: a saved procedure the worker writes in `<run directory>/recipes/` when the method is a rebase and the procedure would work unchanged at a later stop with the same signature. The coordinator keeps accepted entries in `recipes.md` in the run directory.
- A new conflict stop step, Reuse, sends each remaining file whose signature id matches a `recipes.md` line to a new worker step, `steps/reuse.md`, which applies the recipe through `merge.js write`; the coordinator checks the result with `merge.js resolution --partial` and reviews the written files once with no fix rounds. A failed report, untouched file, rejected check, or finding sends those files to `resolve` and removes their recipe line.
- A stop resolved after an escalation saves no recipe.
- `recipe` joins the closed merge role fields in `sai/tools/worker-report-validator.js` (a `{path, signature}` list, completed reports only) and in `sai/policies/worker-report-transport.md`.
- `sai/commands/merge/mechanics.md` documents the conflict signature and the recipe format; `coordinator.md`, `final-correction.md`, `test-correction.md`, `docs/commands/sai-merge.md`, and `AGENTS.md` name the `resolve` step instead of `apply`.
- `sai/install-manifest.json` retires the installed `commands/merge/steps/apply.md` for Claude Code and opencode.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None

### Modified Capabilities

- `sai-merge-command`: one-step worker resolution, the strategy-only escalation path, the conflict signature, and saved-procedure reuse with its single review pass.
- `merge-worker-discipline`: the step pointers name `resolve` and `reuse` instead of `apply`.
- `merge-resolution-transport`: the coordinator hands the accepted `resolve` or `reuse` report path to `merge.js resolution --source`.
- `code-quality-worker-coverage`: the merge code-authoring stage that loads the code-quality policy is `resolve` instead of `apply`.
