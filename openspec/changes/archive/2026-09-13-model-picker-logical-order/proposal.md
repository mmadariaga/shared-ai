## Why
The alphabetical picker buried the entry point and the meta-commands among phase commands, so the list could not be scanned in execution order. Reordering the All table into pipeline phases makes discover, build, review, and close readable in the order they run.

## What Changes
- bin/model-customization.js adds sai-explore to COMMAND_WORKER_ORDER with sai-direct-build-worker, adds ALL_PHASE_ORDER with four fixed phases, and rewrites the All-table grouping to a phased middle block with single blank separators, leftover future commands joining the close phase, orphan workers trailing the close phase, and alphabetical utilities after a blank.
- test/model-customization-menu.test.js reorders COMBINED_BOTH_FULL to the logical pipeline order, updates All-scope separator expectations to four phase gaps, and renames the phased-blocks test from alphabetical to logical order.
- Implemented scope drift: future commands outside the fixed phases join the close phase alphabetically, preserving four groups with no fifth separator.
