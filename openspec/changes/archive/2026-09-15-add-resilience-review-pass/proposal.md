## Why
Unbounded retries and missing timeouts on critical paths cause cascading failures, loss or duplication. Correctness and Performance-triage overlap leaves these gaps without a single owner. The staged implementation gives Resilience single ownership of retries, timeouts, circuit-breaker, idempotency and fallback, with Correctness and Performance-triage trimmed to avoid duplication.

## What Changes
Implemented a dedicated Resilience pass in sai-5-review plus its triage reporting:
- `openspec/schemas/sai-workflow/templates/review.md`: added Resilience Surface Triage section.
- `sai/commands/review/instructions.md`: trimmed Correctness and Performance-triage duplication points; added Resilience as pass 11 (Mutation Analysis becomes pass 12).
- `sai/commands/review/review-report.template.md`: added Resilience Surface Triage with surface, areas and notes.
- `sai/commands/review/steps/resolve-review-analysis.md`: updated to passes 1-10 plus dedicated Resilience pass; Mutation Analysis becomes pass 12.
- `sai/commands/review/worker.md`: executes passes 1-10 plus dedicated Resilience pass before gated Pass 12 mutation.
Scope is external I/O, retryable handlers, consumers and queues, and timeout boundaries; UI files without I/O are exempt. Idempotency applies only when retry, redelivery or double submit is possible. Critical only for cascade, loss or duplication risk with real impact; otherwise High or Medium. No nonexistent library or pattern is required.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- resilience-check: detect unbounded retries, missing timeouts on critical I/O, missing idempotency where retry or redelivery exists, and absent fallback where impact warrants it, with single ownership, scoped surface, conditional idempotency, impact-gated severities, and no-convention restraint.

### Modified Capabilities
None — this change adds a new capability and modifies no existing capability behavior outside review pass ownership and numbering.
