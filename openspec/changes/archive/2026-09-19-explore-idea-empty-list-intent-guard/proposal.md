> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

The `explore-idea@1` machine evaluated its content-based empty-list auto-advance before the intent allowlist. At `review-edge-cases` with a recorded empty edge-case list, or at `implementation-details` with a recorded empty implementation-details list, any signal at all — including an unrecognized intent such as `banana`, the retired `crystallize-resume`, or a conditional-entry intent emitted outside its valid stage set — advanced the progression one stage instead of being rejected.

That silently advanced the progression on turns no caller meant as advancement, and it broke the `A lane intent outside its stage is rejected` scenario of the `Explore-idea intent allowlist` requirement in `openspec/specs/explore-stage-machine/spec.md`. The existing gate test in `test/explore-idea-intent-gate.test.js` only exercised non-empty lists, so the suite stayed green while the scenario was violated. The same behavior reproduces at `git show 1d87ee52:sai-state/machines/explore-idea.js`, so this is a conformance fix rather than a regression repair.

## What Changes

- `sai-state/machines/explore-idea.js` gains an `isIntentless(signal)` helper that treats an absent, `null`, or empty-string `intent` as an intent-less emit.
- Both `shouldAutoAdvance` branches in `transition` are now conditioned on that intent-less check, so the empty-list rule is advancement by content and no longer overrides the intent allowlist. A signal carrying any intent falls through to the allowlist, where an unrecognized or out-of-stage intent is rejected with `READINESS_IS_NOT_INTENT` and an unchanged stage.
- The conditional-entry check still runs ahead of the auto-advance, so a valid `poc-lane-late` at a stage with an empty recorded list still enters the lane.
- Existing outcomes are unchanged for `{}`, `{intent: ''}`, `{intent: 'next-step'}`, a valid conditional entry, and any stage whose own recorded list is non-empty or unrecorded.
- `test/explore-idea-intent-gate.test.js` gains a case covering the empty-list-plus-intent combination at both list stages: `banana`, `crystallize-resume`, and an out-of-stage `poc-lane` are rejected, while the intent-less, empty-string, `next-step`, and valid `poc-lane-late` paths keep their current outcome.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- **explore-stage-machine** — the transition precedence between the content-based empty-list auto-advance and the intent allowlist in `explore-idea@1`: the auto-advance is now reserved for intent-less emits.
- **explore-pre-crystallization-stages** — the user-visible outcome of a non-advancing turn at a stage whose own list was recorded empty: an unrecognized or out-of-stage intent no longer advances the progression.
