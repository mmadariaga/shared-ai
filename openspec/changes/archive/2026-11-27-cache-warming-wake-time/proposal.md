## Why

The line a main session prints on a cache-warming wake with nothing to handle is identical every interval, so the reader cannot tell when the last wait ended or whether consecutive wakes were on schedule. `wait.js` knows the moment its warm result is produced, but the result does not expose it, and the session has no clock without a tool call.

## What Changes

- `skills/universal/sai-cache-warmer/wait.js` adds a top-level `wake_local` field, `HH:mm` in 24-hour local form, to every `warm` result, read once when the result resolves through a dedicated minute formatter; an `arm` result carries none, and the field stays out of `state`, `state_token`, and the chain record.
- `CONTINUATION_REMINDER` in that file names the main-session literal as a template with an `HH:mm` slot and its no-time form.
- `sai/policies/cache-warming.md` defines the main-session literal as `➡️ Warming context cache up - <wake_local>.`, keeps its no-time form `➡️ Warming context cache up.`, lists `wake_local` in the wake payload, and states which parts of the line are fixed text.
- `docs/project-settings.md` documents `wake_local`, the template, and its no-time form.
- `test/cache-warmer.test.js` pins the suite time zone, updates the reminder constant, and asserts `wake_local` on `continue` and immediate `finished` results, its absence from `state`, the state token, the chain record, and the arm check, and its presence in the CLI warm JSON.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `agent-self-warming`: every `warm` result carries the production time `wake_local`; the main-session literal becomes a template with a time slot and a no-time form; the continuation reminder and the literal definitions follow it.
- `explore-handoff-warming`: the hand-off wake turn with nothing to handle prints the same template, with the no-time form as fallback.
- `undelivered-report-recovery`: a coordinator wake with no worker being waited on keeps the main-session literal, now the template; the activity line of a worker wait stays unchanged and carries no time.
- `cache-warming-settings`: the project-settings documentation covers `wake_local` and the template with its no-time form.
