> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

Every pass of the coordinator review-fix loops of `/sai-3-implement` (step review) and `/sai-explore` Direct Build (Step 2 functional fix loop) re-read the whole diff. Unchanged code was reviewed again, fixed findings could be reported again, and problems the change did not cause could open fix rounds. That wasted tokens and rounds and kept the loops from converging.

## What Changes

- New policy `sai/policies/incremental-review.md` defines the pass rules shared by both loops: the first pass reviews the full diff; every later pass reviews only the delta since the previous pass's snapshot plus the direct consumers of the code the delta changed, found through one `budget-explorer` dispatch; an empty delta reviews no code; a missing snapshot or an `n/a` tool verdict falls back to a full review; at most 3 fix rounds, then one final report-only pass over the round-3 delta and its consumers.
- The policy defines a findings ledger with the states `open`, `fixed`, `scope`, and `Pre-existing`. A finding stays open until a later pass confirms it fixed; a `Pre-existing` finding (its problem also exists at the starting point, decided by cause and checked with `git show <ref>:<path>`) is never sent to a worker, never prevents convergence, and is reported once at the end.
- New tool `sai/tools/review-delta.js`: `snapshot [path ...]` writes a Git tree of the working tree through a temporary index in a private directory under the SAI temp root (untracked files included, named ignored paths force-added) without touching the real index, the staging, or the working tree; `delta <from> <to>` returns the changed paths and their diff. Verdicts: `clean`, `changed`, `empty`, `n/a`.
- `sai/commands/implement/coordinator.md` fetches the budget-explorer skill and the policy, takes a snapshot after its `git add` and after each fix round, stores each pass's ledger in `reviews/step-{n}-round-{k}.md` with the snapshot identifier in `reviews/step-{n}-round-{k}.tree`, replaces "read the diff once more" with the final report-only pass, and lists `Pre-existing` findings in the closing report.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md` Step 2 follows the policy with `base_sha` as the starting point, keeps `review_snapshot` and the findings ledger in its conversation-only retained execution state, replaces the "third completed round" cap sentence with the shared cap rule, and adds a `Pre-existing findings` record to the terminal report.
- `sai/install-manifest.json`: the `implement-command`, `build-command`, and `deep-review-command` capability profiles may delegate to the explorer.
- `AGENTS.md` registers the policy and the tool; `docs/commands/sai-3-implement.md` describes the incremental step review; tests cover the tool and the contract wording.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `incremental-review`: the shared pass, ledger, and `Pre-existing` rules of the two coordinator review-fix loops.
- `review-delta-tool`: working-tree snapshots and deltas through a temporary index.

### Modified Capabilities

- `implement-new-adversarial-review`: the step review runs incremental passes with a findings ledger and a final report-only pass.
- `implement-new-run-log`: the closing report lists the `Pre-existing` findings.
- `direct-build-reviews`: the functional fix loop runs incremental passes, shares the `/sai-3-implement` cap rule, retains its snapshot and ledger, and reports `Pre-existing` findings.
- `sai-tools-distribution`: `review-delta.js` joins the registered tool roster.

## Impact

- New files: `sai/policies/incremental-review.md`, `sai/tools/review-delta.js`, `test/review-delta-tool.test.js`.
- Modified files: `AGENTS.md`, `docs/commands/sai-3-implement.md`, `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md`, `sai/commands/implement/coordinator.md`, `sai/install-manifest.json`, `test/implement-family.test.js`.
- `/sai-4-review`, the Direct Build suite-gate rounds (Step 2b), and every other review loop keep their behavior.

Out of scope: design.md, tasks.md, implementation.md — not generated by /sai-backfill
