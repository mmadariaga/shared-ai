## Why

The lightweight probe previously ran immediately before the formal edge-case list (`sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md`), so its conclusions were already baked into the `E` items before the user read them. Showing the list first turns the probes into a visible stress test of the list they refine, which is the comprehension goal of the reorder. The fixed "up to 2 probes before the list" policy was also replaced with a coverage-driven floor (at least 2 scenarios exercised, only material ones shown), silent private correction, and a ripple-explanation rule for user feedback.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md` — the Lightweight probe paragraph was rewritten: the probe now runs after the formal edge-case list has been drafted and before the agreement question; at least 2 scenarios are exercised before anything is shown; only material probes are shown (a probe is material when its outcome adds, removes, or modifies an `E` item, or clarifies a non-obvious consequence of an existing one); holes a probe uncovers are fixed privately so the list is published already coherent, with no change marks and no explanations; each shown probe is a plain direct question with no fixed formula, immediately followed by a self-sufficient prose answer whose `E(N)` citations trail at the end as reference; the search stops when no remaining scenario is material; probes serve only this change's edge-case list.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md` — the Edge-case review gate paragraph now specifies a one-message emission (formal list, then probes, then the single confirmation question, with nothing between), the explicit declaration when fewer than 2 material scenarios exist, and the `- None` short-circuit with no probes run. The `--fast-track` sentence is unchanged.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md` — the answer-evaluation paragraph adds the ripple-explanation rule: explanations of side effects are owed only when user feedback drags changes into `E` items other than the one the feedback addressed, and feedback aimed at a shown probe binds to the `E` item that probe illustrates.
- `test/explore-contract-preservation.test.js` — the contract-preservation allowlist was extended with entries for the two rewritten contract paragraphs (scope drift: this update was not named in the supplied intent).
