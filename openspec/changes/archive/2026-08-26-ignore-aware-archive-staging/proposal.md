## Why

Auto-fast archive staging must safely handle approved paths that are untracked and ignored while retaining eligible paths. Ignored paths are intentional exclusions, not staging failures.

## What Changes

The explicit Auto-fast archive execution path classifies every approved path before staging. Tracked paths, tracked deletions, and untracked non-ignored paths remain eligible for exact-path staging. Approved paths that are both untracked and ignored are omitted with warnings. Classification and real staging errors remain terminal, with no retry or partial commit. Eligible paths continue to the authorized local commit; an all-ignored set preserves the empty-index no-commit behavior. The ordinary archive route remains unchanged.

Regression coverage verifies mixed eligible and ignored paths, all-ignored paths, ordering, allowlist enforcement, and failure handling.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `auto-fast-archive-execution`: classify approved paths before exact staging, omit ignored untracked paths safely, and commit only when eligible paths remain.
