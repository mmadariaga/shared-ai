## Why
Per-Step verification stays green at its own time but later Steps can silently break earlier verified behavior; no surface re-verifies end-to-end today. The staged implementation adds a coordinator-owned terminal functional review that re-exercises the aggregated Human checks after the Step loop and before the Final sweep.

## What Changes
- Extend the run-start Step Projection in sai/commands/apply/coordinator.md to allow exactly one coordinator-derived synthetic terminal entry for the terminal functional review with no heading, no checkbox, and no dispatch, pending until its warnings print and completed when they do.
- Reorder the terminal lifecycle handoff and navigation in sai/commands/apply/coordinator.md so the terminal functional review warnings print before the Final sweep, with lax non-blocking semantics.
- Document the terminal functional review in sai/commands/apply/invocation.md as a screen-only coordinator re-exercise of the aggregated Human checks with fail and unverifiable reasons plus one recommendation line in the user language, with fixed fast-track order of findings, deferred Human Verification list, then the unchanged literal.
- Extend Coordinator Checklist Execution in sai/commands/apply/runner.md so the terminal functional review runs as a read-only extension with no writes, no dispatch, and no verification side effects.
- Add the Terminal functional review section in sai/commands/apply/steps/terminal-lifecycle.md defining coverage, pass and fail and unverifiable verdicts, lax semantics, screen-only reporting, ephemerality, fast-track ordering, and the synthetic projection entry.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- terminal-review: coordinator-run empirical re-verification of aggregated Human checks after the Step loop and before the Final sweep
- terminal-report: screen-only failure and unverifiable warnings with recommendation preceding the unchanged completion literal
### Modified Capabilities
- None — all behavior is introduced as new capabilities; existing apply Step, verification, and completion behavior is unchanged except for the additive pre-sweep review ordering.
