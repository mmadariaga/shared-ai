## Why

The `Review edge cases` and `Implementation details` gates name `next-step` as the advancement action, but a bare `next-step` turn still stalled for a separate explicit confirmation. That broke the announced meaning of the token and added confirmation friction at both list gates. The change aligns the bare token with agreement: at either list gate a bare `next-step` with a non-empty list records the current ordered list as agreed and advances in the same turn, while same-turn revisions, token-hygiene violations, premature `crystallize`, and material-change resets keep their existing safety behavior.
