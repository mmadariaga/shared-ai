## Why

The change-telemetry tool recorded only direct `/sai-plan` and `/sai-build` runs, because the earlier telemetry change excluded the `/sai-explore` routes as a scoping choice. The Plan - Unattended route runs the plan coordinator inside the explore session, which carries no route invocation of its own, so the collector could not find the run while it keyed on a `plan` invocation envelope; the Direct Build route had the same gap, and its route selection pre-authorized only one local commit.

## What Changes

- `sai/tools/change-telemetry.js` gains the in-session commands `sai-explore-plan` and `sai-explore-direct-build`. An in-session route has no invocation envelope, so the collector finds it through the explore root session whose route worker wrote under `openspec/changes/<name>/`: the window starts at the creation of that first worker and ends at the collection, and the latest such session wins. The commands carry their own labels and segment orders (`Spec`, `Design` for the Plan route; `Implement`, `Backfill`, `Archive` for Direct Build), measure the root session under the name `sai-explore`, and report an in-session segment's findings as `unavailable` because the route records no review tally.
- `sai/policies/change-telemetry.md` replaces the exclusion of the explore routes with the in-session route rules: the worker-anchored window, the Plan route collecting at its plan close with its own command and `--run-dir`, and Direct Build collecting after the archive worker's clean `completed` result with `--status completed` and then making the second local commit. A stopped Direct Build run records nothing.
- `sai/commands/plan/coordinator.md`: the Telemetry paragraph passes `--command sai-explore-plan` when the coordinator runs inside the Plan - Unattended route and `--command sai-plan` otherwise.
- `sai/commands/explore/direct-build-coordinator.md` fetches the telemetry policy, collects after the archive worker's clean result and before the terminal report, and makes the second local commit. Its run state records the two confirmed commit references.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/route-selector.md` and `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md` change the Direct Build authorization from one local commit to two: the archived change and its change telemetry.
- `docs/project-settings.md`, `docs/commands/sai-explore.md`, and `AGENTS.md` describe the in-session routes and the two-commit authorization.
- Tests: `test/change-telemetry.test.js` adds an explore-session fixture and coverage for route discovery, the design-only window, the latest-session choice, and the Direct Build segments; `test/change-telemetry-contract.test.js`, `test/direct-build-goal-driven.test.js`, `test/explore-pipeline-selector.test.js`, and `test/explore-question-rounds.test.js` assert the new contract text.
- Scope drift, implemented as reported by the implementer: the documentation updates in `AGENTS.md`, `docs/commands/sai-explore.md`, and `docs/project-settings.md` were not named in the intent statement.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `change-telemetry`: the collector records the in-session explore routes through a worker-anchored window, with their own commands, labels, and segments, and Direct Build records after its archive and makes a second local commit.
- `explore-route-choice`: a Direct Build picker answer pre-authorizes two local commits instead of one.
- `explore-pipeline-selector`: the Direct Build selection and continuation authorizations cover two local commits instead of one.
- `explore-pipeline-supervision`: the Direct Build flow records telemetry after a clean archive result, makes two pre-authorized local commits, and reports both commit references.
- `safe-operations-skill`: the held confirmation names Direct Build's two pre-authorized local commits.
- `decision-record-creation`: the Direct Build records enter the archived-change local commit.
