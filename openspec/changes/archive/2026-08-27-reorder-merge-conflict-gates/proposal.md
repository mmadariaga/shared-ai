## Why

Step 4 of the merge conflict resolution workflow currently fuses evidence gathering, intent reconstruction, alternative construction, and classification into one monolithic block. This creates a logic-ordering defect: classification and the scope gate are held until the entire analysis is already built. There is no seam a cheaper, progressive evidence path can attach to; every downstream performance optimization must carry the entire analysis upfront even when only a portion will be needed.

This change enables architectural simplification by moving source reading and classification to happen immediately after conflict detection, before the language question is answered, and moving the scope gate to run before alternative construction. This creates a seam where future evidence ladders can attach without rebuilding downstream logic.

## What Changes

The merge conflict resolution flow (`sai/commands/merge/instructions.md`, Step 4 and Step 5A) has been reordered:

1. **Moved source reading earlier:** For each conflicted file, the three versions (base, ours, theirs) are now read immediately after conflict detection, before the language question is answered.

2. **Moved classification to early execution:** Classification of files into categories (Artifacts—specs, Artifacts—ADR/DDR, Code) now happens immediately after reading versions, before the language question is answered.

3. **Moved scope gate to Step 5A:** The scope selection gate now runs after the language decision but before alternative construction rather than after all analysis is complete.

4. **Deferred alternative construction:** Complete alternatives (`ours`, `theirs`, `synthesis`) are now constructed in Step 5A after the scope decision, not during Step 4 analysis.
