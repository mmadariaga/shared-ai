**Complexity**: high

## Why

The current `/sai-build` hand-back asks the user to edit a failing test but does not offer authorization for the assistant to try again. The current Step re-entry rule retains spent attempts, so an explicit, bounded grant is needed for that alternative.

## What Changes

- `/sai-4-apply` pauses an exhausted Step before marking it complete, committing, or advancing, and offers manual correction or one explicitly authorized fresh attempt for the whole Step.
- `recovery-ledger@1` accepts the authorized retry event only for the active exhausted Step, archives the exhausted cycle, renews both three-attempt budgets together, and exposes the grant and retained history.
- Ordinary Step re-entry and `--fast-track` do not grant a fresh budget; a second exhaustion requires a new human decision.
- `/sai-build` delegates the same single retry choice to its apply segment without re-presenting it, restarting implementation, or minting another grant.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

No new capabilities.

### Modified Capabilities

- `apply-coordinator-verification`: Add the exhausted-Step choice, explicit authorization, whole-Step retry, and retained attempt-history reporting.
- `apply-same-worker-retry`: Preserve the three-attempt recovery cap per attempt while allowing one explicitly authorized fresh Step attempt.
- `bounded-worker-recovery`: Extend the recovery ledger and emit wire with active-Step retry grants, cycle history, and renewed budgets.
- `sai-build-command`: Delegate the single retry choice to the apply segment without adding a second grant or restarting implementation.
