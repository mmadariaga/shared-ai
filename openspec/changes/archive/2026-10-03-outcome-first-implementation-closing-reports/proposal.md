## Why

Users need to understand whether implementation completed and what needs attention without reading an execution log. This change implements the outcome-first direction of https://github.com/mmadariaga/shared-ai/issues/8 while preserving existing completion conditions and authorization rules.

## What Changes

- Added `sai/policies/implementation-closing-report.md` as the shared closing-format authority for standalone Apply, Build, and Explore's full Direct Build route.
- Defined three required initial status literals and a common order: optional Terminology, What you need to know, complete pending human review when applicable, Next step, and Execution details last.
- Integrated Apply's completion navigation, terminal suite failure close, legacy skipped-suite warning, pending human review, commit records, and autonomous correction traces into that format.
- Integrated Build's successful Apply navigation and invocation-wide failure, cancellation, and interruption close.
- Replaced full Direct Build's four-section presentation while preserving archive, commit, no-push, verification, and incident information.
- Kept original reports and disclosures at commit-authorization decision points; collected final diagnostic records at the end.
- Updated stop-options placement and added or adjusted contract tests for statuses, ordering, route integration, complete human review, authorization preservation, and both harness projections.
- Kept the `--no-specs` POC outside the shared format and left `sai/policies/public-chat.md` unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `implementation-closing-report`: Shared outcome classification, section order, evidence-only reporting, and preservation of authorization visibility for the three full implementation routes.

### Modified Capabilities

- `terminal-report`: Apply's complete pending-human-review block, shared final print order, and reachable stopped suite-gate reporting.
- `apply-completion-clarity`: Existing completion guidance appears in Next step before final diagnostics without changing completion conditions.
- `sai-build-command`: Build reuses Apply's closing navigation and produces one stopped report when implementation fails, is cancelled, or execution is interrupted.
- `explore-pipeline-supervision`: Full Direct Build uses the common closing structure without changing its eight-step lifecycle or mutation ownership.
- `direct-build-terminal-format`: Existing spacing requirements refer to the common closing sections rather than the replaced four-section format.
- `direct-build-suite-gate`: Stopped suite outcomes summarize incompleteness before the technical verification record.
- `unplanned-stop-options`: Existing stop choices follow Execution details as a separate decision, with planned-stop exclusions unchanged.
