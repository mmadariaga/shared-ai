## Why

Plan and Direct Build intents with no crystallized inventory started an anonymous route because startRoute fell back to active current on an empty pending set, letting the selector fire before any Ready to Propose block. The prose emission-then-selector checkpoint could not stop the early emit, and the late block breaks the implementer's block-only input model.

## What Changes

- sai-state/machines/explore-slice.js: startRoute rejects empty pending with NO_PENDING_SLICE, removes ANON_SLICE fallback, starts with pending[0] otherwise; preserves ALREADY_RUNNING precedence and degraded-mode semantics
- sai/commands/explore/steps/route-selector.md: handles NO_PENDING_SLICE as missing inventory, acknowledges no crystallized change, dispatches nothing, prompts block-first
- sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md: clarifies --no-specs POC as direct worker dispatch emitting nothing to explore-slice@1 and bypassing the slice machine entirely
- test/explore-slice-machine.test.js: updates existing tests to record inventory before intent and adds coverage for empty rejection, ALREADY_RUNNING precedence, exhausted set, and selector acknowledgement
