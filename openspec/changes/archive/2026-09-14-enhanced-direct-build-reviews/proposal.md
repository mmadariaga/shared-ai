## Why
Direct Build reviews inferred correctness from the diff and ran project checks when available, otherwise a structural check. That allowed behavior to pass without exercising it and skipped the structural mapping whenever project checks existed. This change strengthens the review by requiring project checks, an always-on structural check, and empirical verification together, improving review of Direct Build results.

## What Changes
- Modified `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md` Step 2 (Functional fix loop): now runs all three checks — (a) project checks when available (standard test/lint/build scripts detected from project metadata); (b) always a structural check (every capability maps to changed paths; every Edge Case is addressed or explicitly contradicted by a constraint); (c) empirical verification that the requested behavior works whenever feasible (exercise the affected flow via the project's runnable surface — tests, CLI, script, or app — rather than inferring it from the diff). Findings formation under artifact-review-contract and fix-round mechanics are unchanged.
