**Complexity**: medium (four instruction surfaces, eight modified capability contracts, no runtime code)

## Why

The active implementation-planning route repeated lifecycle, progress, planning, and validation guidance across coordinator, worker, and step-gated instruction surfaces. That duplication allowed stale or conflicting instructions to cause execution and continuation drift. The change establishes the active worker instruction authority while preserving direct `/sai-3-implement` and `/sai-build` behavior.

## What Changes

- Makes `worker.md`, `steps/common.md`, and the coordinator-selected step file the authoritative technical instruction surface for the active implementation worker.
- Restricts `coordinator.md` to adapter routing, progress rendering, pointer delivery, result validation, and terminal navigation.
- Documents the active-surface boundary for both routed `/sai-3-implement` and the implementation segment of `/sai-build`.
- Removes duplicated generation, delegation, and durable-validation guidance from the worker contract while retaining the required lifecycle and routing contract.
- Makes the canonical validation step the sole technical source for durable implementation-plan verification and audit-step append checks.
- Adds the shared-runner recovery continuation contract to the worker surface.
- Leaves compatibility duplication in `instructions.md` until separate retirement/synergy work accounts for its remaining consumers.
- Leaves `invocation.md` unchanged and does not modify runtime code, wrappers, or build routing.
