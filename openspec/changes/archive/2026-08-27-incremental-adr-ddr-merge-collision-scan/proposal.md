## Why

The merge collision pass scanned all ADR and DDR records after every merge, spending substantial time on unrelated historical records. This change scopes validation to decision records introduced by the merged source branch while preserving protection against newly introduced semantic collisions.

## What Changes

- Capture `target_sha`, `source_sha`, and `merge_base` before the coordinator launches the merge, together with an ordered inventory of added ADR/DDR record paths from the source-versus-base diff.
- Exclude source-side renames and copies, canonical `0000-INDEX.md` files, and introduced records that are absent or unidentifiable in the final merge state.
- Compare only retained source-frontier `(family, numeric prefix)` keys against final-state bare and suffixed ADR/DDR records, processing complete affected groups while leaving unrelated historical collisions untouched.
- Preserve introduction anchor, path, commit, and timestamp metadata; reserve family-aware identifiers before assigning suffixes; and reject occupied rename targets.
- Search and repair repository-wide references only for affected family-aware identifiers, preserving exact links, relationship tokens, index metadata, and ambiguous/orphan escalations.
- Keep the worker read-only and retain merge execution, conflict resolution, renames, reference edits, staging, and commits in the coordinator.
- Update the merge presentation state and documentation, and add contract tests covering incremental collision behavior.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `sai-merge-command`: the post-merge ADR/DDR collision requirement changes from an unconditional global scan to an incremental source-frontier scan with final-state comparison.
- `merge-presentation-seam`: merge provenance, skipped-frontier behavior, affected collision state, and adaptive TODO transitions are extended.
- `merge-collision-metadata`: collision rename plans now carry introduction provenance and collision-free family-aware assignment data.
- `adr-index-maintenance`: suffixed collision repairs preserve exact family-aware index references while limiting work to affected groups.
- `decision-record-index-machinery`: family-aware reference and link invariants apply to the affected identifiers selected by incremental repairs.
