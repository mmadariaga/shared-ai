> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

Technical uncertainty often becomes obvious only while writing edge cases or implementation details, after the automatic go/no-go at the close of stage 1 has already been answered. Before this change the POC lane had exactly one door: the stage-1 entry intent. Declining the go/no-go therefore closed the lane for the rest of the idea's progression, not just the automatic prompt, leaving a user who discovered late uncertainty with no way to discriminate between competing candidates without materially restarting the idea.

## What Changes

- `sai-state/machines/explore-idea.js` — the `CONDITIONAL_ENTRIES` table now maps each entry intent to a **set** of originating stages instead of a single stage, and declares a second entry intent, `poc-lane-late`, valid at `review-edge-cases`, `implementation-details`, and `crystallize`. Both intents enter the same conditional `poc-lane` stage, set `pocLane`, and record no list; the transition guard changed from an equality test to a membership test.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md` — adds the **Late explicit POC entry (stages 2 through 4)** gate, recognized by the same bare-token/dominant-intent machinery that recognizes `next-step`, emitting `{"intent":"poc-lane-late"}` and following the returned `next.follow`. Adds **Agreed lists survive the lane**, stating that a late entry never skips, weakens, or re-opens an agreed `E1..En` or `I1..In` list. The stage-TODO paint rule now describes the five-entry repaint from a later position.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/poc-lane.md` — the lane declares both entry intents and states that its behavior is identical whichever entry brought it there, with no trigger re-evaluation and no go/no-go on a late entry.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md` — crystallization owns no part of the lane; the single exception is a user-authored explicit request during the `Crystallize` stage, which leaves crystallization for the lane, emits no `Ready to Propose` block, and returns to `Review edge cases`.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/slicing-assessment.md` — states that a user who asks for a POC during the stage leaves for the lane and returns through `Review edge cases`, so the assessment runs again afterwards on a repository the lane left as it found it.
- `sai/policies/fast-track-flag.md` — records that the late entry is a user request, not a stop: fast-track neither triggers nor suppresses it.
- `test/explore-idea-intent-gate.test.js`, `test/explore-step-reachability.test.js` — cover late entry from each of the three stages with agreed lists preserved, the return into `review-edge-cases`, rejection at stage 1 and inside the lane, and the ownership of the gate text in `common.md`.
