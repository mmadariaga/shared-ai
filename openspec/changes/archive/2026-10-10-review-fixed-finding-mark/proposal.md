> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

After the Direct Build close of `/sai-review` or `/sai-5-review` commits a fix, the review and audit reports still list the fixed findings, and no consumer can tell them from open ones. Before this change, `sai/commands/review/coordinator.md` left `review.md` as written after a fix, and `/sai-3-implement` audit ingestion (`sai/commands/implement/steps/audit-ingestion.md`) had no notion of a fixed finding, so it could plan already-fixed findings again.

## What Changes

- `sai/policies/finding-state.md` gains § Open and fixed and § Fixed mark. A finding is open without a fixed mark and fixed with one. Reports keep their fixed findings. Every report consumer acts on open findings only. The fixed mark is the literal ` (FIXED)` appended to the end of the finding's heading line. The body, identifier, severity, and closing `Summary:` tally stay unchanged. The mark carries no commit hash, and regenerating a report replaces its findings together with their marks.
- `sai/commands/meta-review/review-fix-worker.md` gains § Fixed marks. After the coordinator accepts convergence, a final `--mark-fixed` continuation lists the source-qualified ids and report paths of the resolved findings. The worker appends the mark to each listed heading line and changes nothing else. An already-marked finding stays as it is. Touched reports are reported in `changed_files`, and the worker writes no further fix.
- `sai/commands/meta-review/direct-build-close.md` adds a fourth fix-loop step, Fixed marks. On convergence only, the close continues the same fix worker once with `--mark-fixed`, answered Questions included. It then reads the diff read-only: the diff may change only those findings' heading lines, and no excluded finding or unanswered Question may be marked. One corrective continuation is allowed. A mark pass that still deviates stages nothing, commits nothing, and ends like non-convergence. The mark pass adds no fix round. The single commit now stages the fix worker's changed-files union (fixed files and marked reports) plus the reports of `input` that the run regenerated. The close runs no test before the commit.
- `sai/commands/review/coordinator.md`: a fix that touches a triage surface leaves the `review.md` triage as written and changes only the fixed marks.
- `sai/commands/implement/steps/audit-ingestion.md`: ingestion ignores every fixed finding, so a fixed finding is never classified Apply, Discard, or Escalate and never appears in the Discarded findings sub-block. The judgment rubric applies to open findings only.
- `sai/commands/implement/steps/rerun-preservation.md`: on a re-run, an audit artifact with no open finding needs no audit step.
- `docs/commands/sai-5-review.md`, `docs/commands/sai-review.md`, and `docs/commands/sai-3-implement.md` describe the fixed mark and the open-findings-only behavior, and state that it holds on Claude Code and opencode.
- `test/review-coordinator-worker.test.js` adds a guard test for the policy, worker, close, implement-step, and documentation wording.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `finding-state`: adds the open and fixed finding states, the ` (FIXED)` fixed-mark form, and its documentation.
- `review-direct-build-close`: adds the fixed-marks pass before the single commit, stages the marked and regenerated reports, and keeps the review triage when a fix touches a triage surface.
- `review-fix-worker`: adds the `--mark-fixed` final continuation.
- `audit-artifact-ingestion`: `/sai-3-implement` ingests open findings only.
- `implement-rerun-preservation`: an audit artifact with no open finding needs no new audit step on re-run.
