> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

The cited-path gate matched only `Local files` headings (`sai/tools/check-cited-paths.js`), while the proposal template writes a bold inline field (`**Local files**:`), so the gate checked no path on sai-1 proposals. The gate never failed because it never saw a path, not because the paths were valid. The missing Purpose section in new capability specs also leaves `TBD` placeholders after archive (329 of 600 live specs in this repository), and `## Additional Notes` invites restating content that already lives in other proposal sections.

## What Changes

- `sai/tools/check-cited-paths.js` extracts cited paths from the `**Local files**:` field: inline comma-separated paths, or bullets below the field until the next heading or bold field. `None`, an empty value, and a placeholder comment yield no path. A path that does not exist is reported with kind `EVIDENCE`. The `#`-`###` `Local files` headings and `Precise file locations` stay accepted. Inline values are split on commas and a trailing note after ` — ` or ` -- ` is ignored.
- `test/check-cited-paths.test.js` (new) covers inline paths, bullets below the field, `None`/empty/placeholder values, and the still-accepted heading forms.
- `openspec/schemas/sai-workflow/schema.yaml`: the `proposal` instruction forbids restating Why, What Changes, Capabilities, Impact, or the research documentation in `Additional Notes` and says to omit the section when nothing remains; the `specs` instruction adds a Purpose rule (new capabilities only: one or two sentences, at least 50 characters; never on a delta of an existing capability).
- `openspec/schemas/sai-workflow/templates/proposal.md`: the `## Additional Notes` comment carries the same no-restating and omit-when-empty rule.
- `openspec/schemas/sai-workflow/templates/specs.md`: starts with a `## Purpose` section and a comment that limits it to new capabilities.
