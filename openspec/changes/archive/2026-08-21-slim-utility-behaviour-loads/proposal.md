> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

Utility sessions paid for behaviour they never used. Four utility bodies (`archive`, `commit`, `pr`, `worktree`) fetched the `budget` skill even though those flows dispatch no subagents, and all seven utility bodies still opened with a five-line `# Isolation Mode` block after the preceding change relocated clean-session enforcement into the harness boot adapters' preamble. The routed spec path repeated the redundancy: its launcher re-carried the `glossary-format` and `budget` directives although worker sessions receive both through their invocation core. Trimming these loads shortens every affected invocation's context without weakening any guarantee — safe-operations stays wherever it was carried, and live-dispatch flows keep their budget load.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/{archive,commit,pr,worktree}/body.md`: drop the `Fetch @skills/budget/SKILL.md` line (no subagent dispatches exist in those flows); keep `Fetch @skills/safe-operations/SKILL.md`.
- All seven utility bodies (`sai/commands/{archive,backfill,commit,explore,pr,status,worktree}/body.md`): delete the leading `# Isolation Mode` block; sessions start clean via the boot-adapter preamble introduced by the previous change.
- `sai/commands/spec/launcher.md`: remove the glossary-format and budget directive lines; keep safe-operations and the spec-worker binding (worker sessions receive budget/glossary discipline via the invocation core).
- New `test/utility-body-slimming.test.js` locks the dieted bodies (no budget, keep safe-operations), the live-dispatch bodies keeping budget (`backfill`, `explore`), the absence of isolation blocks in every utility body, and the exact two-directive spec-launcher content; `test/command-launcher-card.test.js` worst-case spec-launcher array drops to `[safe-operations, binding]`.
- `fixtures/thin-command-wrappers-baseline.json`: the `sai-1-spec` wrapper entries for both harnesses lose the two removed directives.
- `AGENTS.md`: the glossary-format line now states the policy is pre-loaded by each glossary-consuming command via its invocation core (`spec`, `design`, `implement`, `review`) rather than by wrappers at startup.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `utility-body-slimming`: the cross-cutting session-load diet for the seven utility bodies — no isolation blocks anywhere, budget only where the flow dispatches subagents, safe-operations kept wherever carried.

### Modified Capabilities

- `command-launcher-card`: the worst-case spec launcher shrinks from four to two directives (safe-operations plus the spec-worker binding); glossary-format and budget are no longer launcher-borne loads.
- `command-runner-layout`: the boot-preamble no-isolation guarantee now covers utility body cards alongside routed coordinator, invocation, and worker surfaces.
- `worktree-command-distribution`: the shared worktree body opens with its `<TASK>` block instead of the Isolation Mode block and keeps safe-operations as its only behaviour load.
