> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

In an analyzed Plan - Unattended run, three of the four user stops after route selection were decisions that the `Ready to Propose` block had knowingly left open while the user was still present. The spec worker then asked them one by one. The worker asked correctly: the block handed it undecided behavior.

Asking those decisions again at crystallization moves the stops into the attended part of the session. A decision can still stay open, but only as a choice the user made knowingly, after a notice of the consequence.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md` now opens with an open-decision check. An open decision is a behavior the exploration left undefined, whether it sits in an edge case, an implementation detail, or the conversation. Only when at least one exists does the file fetch the new step and complete it before the slicing assessment is loaded. With none, nothing is shown.
- New step file `sai/commands/explore/steps/open-decisions.md`:
  - shows one pinned consequence notice once per idea, before the first question;
  - asks each open decision once, one decision per question, as a closed-choice prompt through the native picker, with that decision's own answers and `Leave it open` as the last option;
  - records one of two outcomes per decision, in the conversation only: an answered decision goes to `Decisions & Rationale` and rewrites the `Edge Cases` or `Implementation Details` item that held it, and a decision left open goes to `Request Additional Notes` in the `Undecided:` form;
  - asks the same decision again after an ambiguous, missing, or dismissed reply;
  - hands a reply that materially changes the idea to the existing material-change rule;
  - runs once per idea, attributes each outcome to the right block when the idea is sliced, and does not repeat recorded outcomes or the notice when crystallization resumes.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/slicing-assessment.md` and `sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md` state the new Crystallize entry order: open decisions when any exist, then the slicing assessment, then the crystallization language gate, then block printing. `common.md` also accepts a pending open-decision question as the stage 4 turn closure, re-asked after an interruption.
- `sai/policies/ready-to-propose-format.md` declares `Request Additional Notes` the single place of the block where an open decision travels, one `Undecided:` sentence per decision. Every other field states decided behavior only. The block gains no field.
- `sai/policies/fast-track-flag.md` and `AGENTS.md` state that the open-decision reconfirmation runs unchanged under `--fast-track`.
- Tests pin the new texts and the two new rules.

The `explore-idea@1` stage machine is unchanged: no new stage, event, or panel entry. `sai/tools/ready-to-propose.js` is unchanged. The auto-answer rule of the Plan - Unattended route and the worker question rules are not changed.

Known limitations:

- Crystallization takes longer when open decisions exist.
- A decision the user leaves open still stops the unattended route later. The change guarantees the warning, not the absence of the stop.
- Detection of an open decision is a judgment of the explore session, with no formal marker.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `explore-open-decision-reconfirmation`: the conditional step at `Crystallize` entry that asks each open decision again and requires an explicit per-decision choice to leave one open.

### Modified Capabilities

- `shared-crystallization-block-format`: adds the rule that `Request Additional Notes` is the single carrier of open decisions, in the `Undecided:` form.
- `sai-fast-track-flag`: adds the rule that explore's open-decision reconfirmation runs unchanged under `--fast-track`.
