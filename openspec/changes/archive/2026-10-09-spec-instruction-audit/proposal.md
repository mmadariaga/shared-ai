> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

The `/sai-1-spec` path carried instruction that changed no behavior: the proposal `**Complexity**` token had no operational consumer, yet cost the worker a provisional write and a rubric derivation on every run, and several worker and step sentences restated `sai/orchestration/worker-core.md` rules or coordinator-only duties, adding context load. Issue #48 audited this over-instruction; this change applies its resolved decisions.

## What Changes

- Retire Proposal Complexity generation: remove the provisional `**Complexity**` write from `sai/commands/spec/steps/proposal.md`, and the `## Complexity Derivation Rubric` section plus its "derive the complexity token" mentions from `sai/commands/spec/steps/validation.md`, renumbering the validation sequence to three items.
- Remove the `**Complexity**` instruction from the proposal artifact in `openspec/schemas/sai-workflow/schema.yaml` and the Complexity line and comment from `openspec/schemas/sai-workflow/templates/proposal.md`, which now starts at `## Why`.
- Remove the **Proposal Complexity** glossary entry, its three invariants, and the "Change-level vs step-level complexity" ambiguity entry from `GLOSSARY.md`; remove the Proposal Complexity mentions from `AGENTS.md` and `docs/sequential-pipeline.md`. **Routing Complexity** in `tasks.md` is unchanged.
- Trim `sai/commands/spec/worker.md`: delete the sentence restating that progress events are returned lifecycle results and coordinator-only duties; reduce the post-resolution failure paragraph to a pointer to `worker-core.md` § Failure classification plus the spec-specific evidence rule; reduce the forbidden-write bullet to its `blocking-contradiction` mapping; delete the bullet about remaining worker-core classes and the recovery persistence sentence. The `generation-error`, `validation-failed`, and `blocking-contradiction` mapping and the `continue_after_recovery` correction flow stay.
- Trim `sai/commands/spec/steps/research.md` (Research Leads are "starting points"; the "When Research Leads are absent… research normally" bullet is removed) and `sai/commands/spec/steps/proposal.md` (step 4 reads "Stop there: `specs/**` is the next step.").
- Archive DDR 0072 and DDR 0156a to `docs/ddr/archive/`, moving their entries to the historical section of `docs/ddr/0000-INDEX.md`.
- Add ADR 0191, which **Amends** ADR 0172c: a spec-phase restatement of another file's rule is kept only when a decision record or an observed failure justifies it; the duplication invariant and its dependency on DDR 0156a are retired. ADR 0172c is left unedited and the ADR index records the amendment.
- Tests: drop the assertions on the trimmed worker sentences and the Complexity Derivation Rubric heading, drop the two Proposal Complexity documentation assertions in the design test, and add a test that the rubric, the proposal-step Complexity write, the template Complexity line, and the schema Complexity instruction are gone.
