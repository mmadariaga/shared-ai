**Complexity**: medium (14 files, docs-only, no breaking change)

## Why

Creating slice-crystallization and reviewed items at block emission invents a task list before the implementation route is known. Deferring the path selector until crystallization concludes keeps the panel empty until slice plus mode resolve.

## What Changes

- Crystallization now ends at the Ready to Propose block plus `---` with the stage TODO cleared and no selector reference, in `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md` and `sai/commands/explore/instructions.md`.
- A new deferred route choice in `sai/commands/explore/steps/route-selector.md` runs when crystallization has concluded via `explore-slice@1` idle and `next.follow`, before any route dispatch.
- `sai-state/machines/explore-slice.js` maps idle to the new route-selector step with no machine version bump; `sai/commands/explore/steps/slice.md` keeps only inventory wiring.
- The idea progress list in `sai/commands/explore/steps/idea-list.md` plus both harness render bindings now holds only route-owned entries with first render at choice resolution and an empty panel from emission until resolution.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-selector.md` retains only supervision state; presentation, deterministic choice, and route entries live in the new selector step.
- Supervised and manual review surfaces in `sai/commands/explore/steps/review-loop.md`, `pipeline-plan-unattended.md`, `pipeline-direct-build.md`, and `sai/commands/design/worker.md` form findings only with old reviewed references inert.
- Slicing assessment in `sai/commands/explore/steps/slicing-assessment.md` records change names for the later choice and adds no panel entries.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `deferred-selector`: State-machine-served route choice presented when crystallization has concluded, before any route dispatch.
- `route-owned-todo`: Selected path creates its own task list with no baseline evidence items.

### Modified Capabilities

- `crystallization-close`: Crystallization close now ends at block emission with no selector reference.
