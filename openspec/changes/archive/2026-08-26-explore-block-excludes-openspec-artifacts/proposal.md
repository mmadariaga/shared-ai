## Why

OpenSpec bookkeeping artifacts (`openspec/changes/{name}/**`, `openspec/specs/**`) are consequences of the explore workflow owned by backfill and archive workers. The boundary between explore-owned decisions and archive-owned artifact management belongs at block-authoring time, not only as a worker permission in the autofast implementer, where it arrives after the block is already crystallized.

## What Changes

Modified the explore workflow to exclude OpenSpec bookkeeping artifacts from `**Implementation Details**` and `**Capabilities in scope**` at block-authoring time:

1. **`sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md` stage 3**: Added authoring-scope rule stating that decisions whose object is a bookkeeping artifact (`openspec/changes/{name}/**`, `openspec/specs/**`) do not qualify as `I` items.

2. **`sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md`**: Added explicit statement that OpenSpec bookkeeping artifacts are excluded from `**Capabilities in scope**`, and clarified that `**Why**`, `**Decisions & Rationale**`, and `**Research Leads**` are exempt from this exclusion.

3. **`sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-auto-fast.md`**: Rewrote the pre-dispatch compatibility refusal clause to narrow its scope to the step-1 implementer's own work only (planning-artifact creation, mutating git commands, subagent dispatch, and writes under `openspec/` designated as step-1 work). Added a bounded pre-dispatch recovery cycle for blocks targeting non-bookkeeping content under `openspec/`. Steps 3–8 (backfill and archive) own writes to OpenSpec bookkeeping artifacts.
