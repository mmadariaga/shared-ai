## Why
Unify startup behavior between routed workers and explore: pre-crystallization sai-explore budget-explorer dispatches ran single-phase with goal and output contract in the initial spawn, skipping the event ready withholding defined in sai/orchestration/worker-core.md. Adding a minimal two-phase handshake brings explore research spawns under the same ready discipline as routed stretches without changing the worker-core contract.
