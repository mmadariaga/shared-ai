> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

The experimental build family (`/sai-build-new`, `/sai-3-implement-new`, `/sai-4-review-new`) proved itself, so keeping two parallel pipelines only added maintenance cost and user confusion; `AGENTS.md` documented the coexistence as temporary. This change retires the legacy family and gives the new family the stable command names.

## What Changes

- **Legacy family retired.** `/sai-4-apply` and `/sai-5-review` are deleted, together with the old `/sai-build` composition (`sai/commands/meta-build/`), the old implementation planner (`sai/commands/implement/` planning cards and steps), the apply cards (`sai/commands/apply/`), the old review command bootstrap, the `implement-standalone`, `apply-standalone`, and `review-standalone` machines, the `sai-3-implementation-worker`, `sai-4-red-worker`, `sai-4-green-worker`, `sai-5-review-worker`, and `sai-review-fix-worker` agents, their wrappers in `commands/{claude,opencode}/`, their `docs/commands/` pages, and the tests that covered only those pieces.
- **New family promoted.** `implement-new/` moves to `sai/commands/implement/`, `review-new/` to `sai/commands/review/`, and `build-new/` to `sai/commands/build/`; `/sai-3-implement`, `/sai-4-review`, and `/sai-build` now run the former `-new` contracts. The old `review/review-report.template.md` and `review/options.md` stay in the promoted review directory.
- **`-new` suffix removed.** Workers become `sai-3-red-worker`, `sai-3-green-worker`, and `sai-4-review-worker`; machines become `implement-run@1`, `red-steps@1`, `green-steps@1`, and `review-steps@1`; the implement session key becomes `implement-{name}`. Every registry is updated: `sai/install-manifest.json`, `bin/worker-matrix.js`, `bin/model-customization.js`, `bin/orchestration-source-audit.js`, `bin/install-flow.js`, `bin/install-manifest.js`, `bin/sai-state.js`, `sai-state/registry.js`, `sai/tools/lint.js`, the presets, and `fixtures/thin-command-wrappers-baseline.json`.
- **Install manifest.** The implement command declares its own shell and write grants instead of extending `apply-coordinator`; new `build-command`, `review-command`, and `deep-review-command` capability profiles replace the legacy ones; `retirements` lists every removed or renamed wrapper, agent, worker binding, capability file, and command card.
- **`/sai-review` renamed to `/sai-deep-review`.** The new `sai/commands/deep-review/coordinator.md` runs `/sai-4-review` as its review segment, maps its `no-changes` result to a clean stop, activates the recommended audits from the triage parse, then runs the findings round (`findings-selection.md`), marks unselected fixable findings `EXCLUDED`, and fixes the selected findings through a `/sai-3-implement` segment. `direct-build-close.md` and `review-fix-worker.md` are removed.
- **Planning artifact retired.** `openspec/schemas/sai-workflow/schema.yaml` drops the `implementation` artifact and `templates/implementation.md`, the review and audit artifacts no longer require it, and the apply block becomes `requires: [tasks]`, `tracks: tasks.md`. `sai/tools/status.js` reads task progress from `tasks.md` and routes to `/sai-3-implement` and `/sai-4-review`. `sai/commands/archive/instructions.md` drops its `implementation.md` completion check and counts ten artifacts.
- **Direct Build suite gate inlined.** `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md` states the terminal suite gate stop itself instead of citing `/sai-4-apply`.
- **Tool consolidation.** `parseDeclaration` and `declarationsOverlap` move from `sai/tools/apply-step.js` into `sai/tools/file-manifest.js`; `apply-step.js` is deleted.
- **Policies and prose.** `sai/policies/finding-state.md` names `/sai-deep-review` and `/sai-3-implement` and drops the rule that the deep-review close ignores `EXCLUDED` marks; `sai/policies/implementation-closing-report.md` loses its apply-only `apply-step.js inspect` and learnings-promotion parts; the remaining policies, adapters, `AGENTS.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`, `README.md`, and the docs pages name the surviving commands.
- **Tests.** `test/implement-new-family.test.js` becomes `test/implement-family.test.js` and asserts the old files are absent; `test/replace-legacy-build-family.test.js` is added; the remaining suites follow the renames.
