**Complexity**: high (6 capabilities; shared protocol change across both harnesses)

## Why

Separate progress-panel turns repeatedly process the coordinator's long context. Validated, recorded progress can independently drive panel updates and worker continuation in one assistant turn.

## What Changes

- Changed the shared runner to validate and register progress before issuing independent panel updates and same-worker continuation in one turn.
- Updated Claude Code and opencode panel bindings, progress policy, and six coordinator cards to use this progress-only exception.
- Preserved initial render-first ordering, step-level panels, validator-sourced milestone stamps, just-in-time pointers, coordinator ownership, and existing no-commit guard boundaries.
- Added regression coverage for same-turn ordering, panel degradation, and preserved combined startup, early outcomes, design variants, and reconstruction.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `harness-panel-render-binding`: native same-turn progress updates and continuation on both harnesses.
- `non-blocking-runtime-panel-tool-degradation`: progress-result handling without duplicate continuation; unchanged one-time degradation.
- `orchestration-core`: register progress before independent presentation and continuation; preserve combined startup.
- `progress-render-act`: state-changing progress updates share a turn with continuation.
- `runner-pointer-extension`: pointers derive from registered routing state independently of panel results.
- `todo-list-progress-rendering`: preserve step-level updates without requiring panel completion before continuation.
