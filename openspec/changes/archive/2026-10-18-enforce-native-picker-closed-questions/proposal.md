> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

In a retrospective session the Explore coordinator asked closed-choice questions and publication approvals as plain text, even after the user asked twice for the native option widget. The "Closed-choice prompts" rule lived only in `sai/policies/remember.md` and was absent or contradicted at the points of use: `skills/universal/to-backlog/SKILL.md` said only "Offer confirm, edit, and cancel", and `skills/universal/from-backlog/SKILL.md` let the unresolved question use "the ordinary conversation or closed-choice mechanism". The failure is rule salience at the point of use, not a missing rule, so this change restates the picker rule where each closed-choice question is asked and loads the question-format policy at Explore start.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/explore/body.md`: the Explore load sequence now fetches `sai/policies/question-context.md` right after `sai/policies/remember.md`, so the question-format policy is in context before Explore's first question.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md`: a new stage-independent paragraph, "Closed-choice questions (every stage)", states that at every Explore stage, including a discovery question that offers options, a question whose answer is one of a finite listed option set goes through the harness-native picker per the "Closed-choice prompts" rule in `remember.md`, naming `AskUserQuestion` on Claude Code and `question` on opencode, with free text still available. A set larger than the picker capacity falls back to plain text that keeps every option in order, and an open question stays plain text. Stage-navigation questions (the maturity question that offers moving to `Review edge cases`, and the `List stages` agreement questions) keep their plain text form; the optional native stage selectors stay governed by their own existing rule in the same file.
- `skills/universal/to-backlog/SKILL.md`: the publication confirmation now offers confirm, edit, and cancel through the harness-native picker (Claude Code's `AskUserQuestion`, opencode's `question`), and the standalone re-ask after a mismatch or missing answer is presented through the harness-native picker.
- `skills/universal/from-backlog/SKILL.md`: the first unresolved question after loading an item now chooses its channel by shape: a question with a closed option set goes through the native picker (`AskUserQuestion` on Claude Code, `question` on opencode), and an open question uses the ordinary conversation.
- All edits are in the shared sources (`sai/`, `skills/universal/`), so Claude Code and opencode receive the same behavior through their installs.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `explore-closed-choice-questions`: closed-choice questions asked during Explore, including discovery questions with options, use the harness-native picker named locally per harness; open questions and stage-navigation questions stay plain text; the question-format policy loads at Explore start.
- `backlog-skill-confirmations`: the `to-backlog` publication confirmation and its standalone re-ask, and the `from-backlog` closed-choice unresolved question, use the harness-native picker.

### Modified Capabilities

- None
