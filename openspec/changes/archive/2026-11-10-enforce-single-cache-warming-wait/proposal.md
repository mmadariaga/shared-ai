## Why

Waits of the same chain overlap today because a new arm only bumps the chain generation; the older wait sleeps its full interval and then returns `finished`, costing an extra wake turn per overlap (skills/universal/sai-cache-warmer/wait.js:210-216, skills/universal/sai-cache-warmer/wait.js:362-367).

## What Changes

- `skills/universal/sai-cache-warmer/wait.js` gains a foreground `arm --chain <id>` check that prints one JSON decision, `{"launch": true|false}`. After each response the agent runs the check and launches a background `warm --chain <id>` only when the answer is `true`; an `arm` without `--chain` exits 2.
- The check reuses a live wait whose owner lease is fresh, whose resolved interval and max match the record, and whose arm instant is not earlier than the cutoff mark. It extends that wait's window to `now` plus `max` and answers `false`. It answers `true` when no wait owns the chain, the lease is stale, the timings changed, or the cutoff mark is newer. It answers `false` when warming is off or a fixed-deadline chain is at or past its deadline.
- `warm --chain` takes ownership under an exclusive lock file created with `wx`, with a random owner token and a bumped generation. A new owner waits until the previous owner releases its lease or the lease goes stale (three renewal ticks of 3 seconds) before it starts its interval, so a replacement never waits a full interval for the old wait to end.
- A live wait sleeps in chunks of `TICK_MS` (3 seconds) and, after each chunk, renews its lease and re-reads the generation and closing instant. A wait that loses ownership, sees a newer cutoff mark, or cannot renew retires with `finished` within one chunk and clears its lease. A lock file older than 10 seconds is removed, so a crashed writer never blocks the chain.
- A wake re-arm keeps ownership only while its generation is still current and its arm instant is not earlier than the cutoff mark. It resolves the interval again and keeps the chain's closing instant.
- An ordinary `begin` of a chain that holds a live wait keeps that wait's owner lease, interval, max, and closing instant, so the next arm check reuses the running wait. Only a `--fixed-deadline` begin clears them and sets the new deadline.
- A fixed-deadline hand-off chain reuses its live wait through the check, never moves the deadline, and launches nothing after the deadline.
- `sai/policies/cache-warming.md` states the check-then-launch protocol once for Claude Code and opencode and describes retirement within seconds. `sai/policies/explore-agent.md` allows the three `wait.js` commands, adding the foreground arm check. `sai/commands/explore/steps/handoff-warming.md` runs the check before launching the hand-off wait and holds a task id only when a wait launched.
- `docs/project-settings.md` and `AGENTS.md` describe the foreground arm check, the single live wait, and retirement within seconds.
- `test/cache-warmer.test.js` covers the reuse and launch decisions, stale leases, changed timings, cutoff marks, fixed deadlines, concurrent arms, ordinary and fixed-deadline begins, stale lock recovery, and a spawned-process test that proves the check reuses the one live wait and a replacement retires it.
