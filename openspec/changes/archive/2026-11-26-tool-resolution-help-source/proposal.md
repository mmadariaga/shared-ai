> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

`sai/policies/tool-resolution.md` restated by hand the invocation forms of about 15 tools. Once every agent-facing tool answers `<tool> [<subcommand>] --help` with its usage and examples, that catalog duplicated a source of truth the environment already holds, and it drifted whenever a tool changed its flags.

## What Changes

- `sai/policies/tool-resolution.md` names the tool's own help as the source of an invocation form: when an agent is unsure of the form of a call, or a call ends with a usage error, it runs `node <tool-path> <subcommand> --help` (`node <tool-path> --help` for a tool without subcommands) and uses the usage and examples it prints. `to-pr.js`, `to-backlog.js`, and `from-backlog.js` stay called as their owning universal skill defines.
- The catalog of per-tool invocation forms is removed from `sai/policies/tool-resolution.md`.
- A new `## Results` section keeps what `--help` does not carry: the verdicts, printed fields, and exit codes of `scenario-coverage.js`, `test-select.js`, `risk-signals.js`, `change-document.js`, `change-telemetry.js`, `decision-records.js`, and `backfill-no-specs.js`; which tools take no `--cwd` or `--json` (`check-delta-headers.js`, `tool-access.js`, the `bin/sai-state.js` verbs); and the `bin/sai-state.js` `emit` rules (flags for identifier events, `--input-file` for free text or rejected paths, the progress emit loading the validator itself).
- The missing-tool rule moves under its own `## When a tool does not resolve` heading, unchanged. The structured-input `--input-file` rule and the `root: project` / `root: global` rule stay unchanged.
- Command cards and every other policy keep their exact invocation lines. `test/change-telemetry-contract.test.js` now checks that the policy keeps what `change-telemetry.js` prints and no longer carries its `collect` invocation form, and reads the `explore-origin` invocation form from `sai/policies/change-telemetry.md`, where it already lives.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None

### Modified Capabilities

- `shared-tool-resolution`: the tool-resolution policy takes invocation forms from each tool's `--help` and keeps only the meaning of results; the decision-record inventory and risk-signals requirements no longer require the policy to declare their invocation forms.
- `change-telemetry`: the policy states what `collect` and `explore-origin` print instead of their invocation forms; the `explore-origin` form lives in `sai/policies/change-telemetry.md`.
- `sai-tools-distribution`: the policy states what `scenario-coverage.js` and `test-select.js` print instead of listing their invocation forms.
- `design-file-manifest-tool`: the policy lists `file-manifest.js` by name and leaves its invocation form to `--help` and the cards.
- `block-file-delivery`: the policy carries no `change-document.js` invocation form; the file form of `check` stays required at every prescribing instruction.
- `dead-review-step-retirement`: the absence of `validate-findings.js` is checked against the whole policy, which no longer has a per-tool invocation list.
