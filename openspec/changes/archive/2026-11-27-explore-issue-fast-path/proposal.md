> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

`/sai-explore <issue-url>` on a mature issue (`Maturity: Ready to propose`) cost about four model turns and two explorer dispatches after the base load, and the intake repeated work: the project assessment and the drift check validated the same thing, the full issue text was shown in chat and again in the crystallized document, the model recomposed a document that is the issue text plus two `## Change` lines, and a re-imported published change document was sliced again although its origin exploration already sliced it. The work question and the drift check interrupted the user in two separate rounds, and the contract contradicted itself: the work question had to read maturity from `change-document.js sections` while `sections` ran only after the work answer, so the model judged maturity itself.

## What Changes

- The issue intake runs `change-document.js sections` once, as soon as the complete issue content is in the conversation and before asking, and takes maturity and the proposed change name from that reading.
- A new deterministic precheck, `sai/tools/issue-precheck.js`, lists the backticked paths, `path:line` references, and symbols the working tree does not hold, each with the section where it appears. The precheck informs and never decides.
- A mature issue skips the from-backlog §3 full presentation and §4 project assessment inside `/sai-explore`, presents a summary, and asks the work question and the drift check in one round of `sai/policies/question-rounds.md`.
- A new `change-document.js compose` subcommand writes the change document from the issue's literal text and the settled `## Change` lines. The crystallization then runs `check`, judges hygiene only for the lines it flags, emits the document, and shows the route selector.
- A re-imported published change document, recognized by a reading that returns `change.name`, skips the slicing assessment.
- `sai/policies/tool-resolution.md`, `docs/commands/sai-explore.md`, and `AGENTS.md` document the new tool behavior and the fast path.
- Tests cover `compose`, the precheck, the one-round intake, the skipped presentation and assessment, and the tool usage declaration.

- `issue-url-entry`: an imported mature issue skips the from-backlog full presentation and project assessment and asks the work question and the drift check in one round.
