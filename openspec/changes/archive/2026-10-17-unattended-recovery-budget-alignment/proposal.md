## Why

The unattended recovery policy gave every failed step three attempts, so the Plan diagnosis counter allowed three attempts while the Plan route and the `explore-pipeline-supervision` spec allow one diagnosis round per phase. Direct Build also counted attempts per scope (`direct-build`, `backfill`, `archive`), against the per-step rule of the `unattended-resilience-rule` spec. Each route now has one budget per scope that matches its spec.

## What Changes

- `sai/policies/unattended-runtime-recovery.md` § Attempts states one budget per scope, shared between the two recovery paths: one diagnosis round per Plan phase, and three attempts per failed Direct Build step.
- Plan - Unattended: `diagnosis_rounds.spec` or `diagnosis_rounds.design` allows one diagnosis round for the active phase. The Explore Diagnosis Round and its Bounded Recovery continuation form that one round and are charged once on the counter.
- Direct Build - Unattended: `diagnosis_rounds.direct_build` is keyed by the failed step (`step-1`, `step-2`, `step-2b`, and `step-3` through `step-8`) instead of the `direct-build`, `backfill`, and `archive` scopes. Each failed step has three attempts, and a step's attempts never draw on another step's key.
- The stop condition now stops when the Plan phase's diagnosis round is used with the step still failing, when a Direct Build step's counter reaches three with that step still failing, or when the budget agent repeats the previous diagnosis.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md` initializes `diagnosis_rounds.direct_build` on a new slice with one counter per step, each at `0`, and keeps the counters across a same-slice resume.
- `test/unattended-runtime-recovery.test.js` asserts the new per-phase and per-step wording and rejects the old per-scope keys.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None

### Modified Capabilities

- `unattended-resilience-rule`: the attempt budget is one diagnosis round per Plan phase and three attempts per failed Direct Build step.
- `bounded-worker-recovery`: the shared diagnosis allowance charges one round per Plan phase and keys Direct Build counters per failed step.
- `explore-pipeline-supervision`: the Direct Build run state keeps per-step attempt counters, and archive failure correction uses the attempts of the failed archive step.
