## Why
Review quality scales with the orchestrator model while audit workers are cost-sensitive per task, so each review phase now uses the most cost-effective model for its task. The previous defaults pointed every review phase at `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash`, which under-provisioned orchestration and over-provisioned cost-insensitive workers.

## What Changes
On the opencode harness only, the four review orchestrators move from `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash` with `variant: max` to `opencode-go/muse-spark-1.3-contributor` with `variant: xhigh`: `commands/opencode/sai-5-review.md`, `commands/opencode/sai-6-security.md`, `commands/opencode/sai-7-performance.md`, and `commands/opencode/sai-8-accessibility.md` (frontmatter model plus variant, two lines each). The worker-matrix in `sai/install-manifest.json` moves three opencodeAgent entries: `sai-5-review-worker` from `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash` to `opencode-go/gpt-5.6-luna` with `variant: max` unchanged, `sai-7-performance-worker` and `sai-8-accessibility-worker` from `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash` to `opencode-go/glm-5.3-flash` with `variant: max` unchanged. `sai-6-security-worker` stays on `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash` with `variant: max` as an intentional no-op, and the `sai-review` orchestrator is likewise untouched. `commands/claude/**` is untouched by explicit scope decision. Model IDs and `xhigh`/`max` variants are applied exactly with the `opencode-go/` prefix and no silent substitution. Repo source only; installed copies under `~/.config/opencode` pick the change up after a later reinstall/doctor outside this change.

### Modified Capabilities
