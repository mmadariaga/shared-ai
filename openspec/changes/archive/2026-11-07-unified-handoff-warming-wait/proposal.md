## Why

The `/sai-explore` hand-off wait restated its own arm, re-arm, and deadline rules in prose (`sai/commands/explore/steps/handoff-warming.md` and the § Hand-off wait section of `sai/policies/cache-warming.md`). Those rules duplicated the warming protocol that `wait.js` now owns, so the hand-off wait was a second protocol to maintain. This change makes the hand-off wait an ordinary `wait.js` chain whose window is a fixed deadline held by the tool.

## What Changes

- `skills/universal/sai-cache-warmer/wait.js`: `begin` accepts a valueless `--fixed-deadline` switch. The switch is valid only for the main session and only together with an explicit `--interval` and `--max`; otherwise `begin` rejects it as a usage error. With the switch, `begin` stores `deadline_ms` in the chain record, `max` minutes after that `begin`. A later `begin` without the switch rewrites the record with no deadline.
- `wait.js warm`: on a chain whose record holds a deadline, an arm supersedes the earlier wait but keeps the window's closing instant at the deadline, and an arm at or after the deadline returns `finished` with `waited_ms` 0 and opens no new generation.
- `wait.js` flag parsing accepts valueless switches per subcommand; `warm` accepts none.
- `sai/policies/cache-warming.md` § Hand-off wait: the hand-off wait is an ordinary main-session chain with a fixed deadline that `wait.js` owns. The command settles the timings (the `agent` setting or the user's answers) and always passes them to `begin` with `--interval`, `--max`, and `--fixed-deadline`. The timings stay as passed to `begin` at every wake. The prose rules for the variant ("only a wake re-arms", re-resolving setting timings at the wake) are removed.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/handoff-warming.md`: `begin` always receives `--interval <interval> --max <max> --fixed-deadline` with the settled timings, and the step file no longer describes its own deadline and re-arm variant.
- `test/cache-warmer.test.js`: coverage for the fixed deadline in `begin` and `warm`, and for the updated policy and step-file text.

Scope drift (not an implementation error): `begin` rejects `--fixed-deadline` without explicit `--interval` and `--max`, and rejects it together with `--agent`. The selected diff evidences this behavior, but the supplied intent statement did not name it.

Scope drift (not an implementation error): a later `begin` of the same main-session chain without `--fixed-deadline` rewrites the chain record with no deadline. The selected diff evidences this behavior, but the supplied intent statement did not name it.

Scope drift (not an implementation error): an arm at or after the fixed deadline returns `finished` with no new chain generation. The selected diff evidences this behavior, but the supplied intent statement did not name it.

### Modified Capabilities

- `explore-handoff-warming`: the hand-off close always passes the settled timings and `--fixed-deadline` to `begin`, and the deadline is set by that `begin`.
