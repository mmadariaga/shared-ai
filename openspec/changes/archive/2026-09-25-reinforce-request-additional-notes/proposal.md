> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

The `**Request Additional Notes**` field rule in `sai/policies/ready-to-propose-format.md` had a vague trigger ("fits no other field") and was dominated by prohibitions. In one observed run, crystallization dropped agreed future-behavior detail: a non-goal in `**Key constraints**` looked like it already covered that detail, so the "never duplicate" rule suppressed the field. The rule now says in positive terms what the field holds, separates a non-goal from its detail, and adds a checkable emission sweep that runs before the block is printed.

## What Changes

- The `**Request Additional Notes**` bullet under `## Field rules` in `sai/policies/ready-to-propose-format.md` is rewritten. The new rule defines the field as discussed, agreed context that could influence implementation and fits no other field. It lists three non-exhaustive examples: future behavior agreed on but excluded from this change; related follow-up work such as PBIs or tickets; and any other agreed context of this kind, including points left explicitly undecided.
- The rule separates a non-goal from its detail. The non-goal is a binding exclusion and goes to `**Key constraints**`. Its detail (how the excluded behavior should work later) goes to the notes field, and the pair is not duplication. A requirement that describes future behavior outside this change goes to the notes as future context. An obligation of this change still goes to `**Edge Cases**`, `**Implementation Details**`, or `**Key constraints**`.
- The rule adds an **Emission sweep**, run before the block is printed. It is complete when every discussed-and-excluded topic whose detail goes beyond its non-goal in `**Key constraints**` has that detail in `**Request Additional Notes**`. When no content meets this criterion or the definition, the field is omitted.
- The prohibitions are restated as guardrails, each paired with a positive target:
  - each fact is carried once, in the field that owns it;
  - the field is either emitted with content or omitted, never empty or as `- None`;
  - each per-slice block sweeps only its own slice's content and carries only its own slice's notes;
  - the `/sai-3-implement` escalation block may include the field but is not required to.
- A generic, illustrative block fragment follows the guardrails. It shows a non-goal in `**Key constraints**` and its future detail in `**Request Additional Notes**`.
- The consumer sentence ("Consumers treat it as informative context only…") and the block-template placeholder are unchanged.
- New `test/ready-to-propose-notes-rule.test.js` pins the positive definition and examples, the non-goal/detail boundary, the emission-sweep criterion and per-slice sweep, the illustrative example, and the unchanged consumer sentence.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

- Modified: `sai/policies/ready-to-propose-format.md` (only the `**Request Additional Notes**` field rule).
- New: `test/ready-to-propose-notes-rule.test.js`.
- Unchanged:
  - block structure, field order, field label, and the field's optional and non-normative status;
  - `sai/commands/explore/steps/crystallization-protocol.md` and `crystallization-language-gates.md`;
  - `sai/tools/lint.js`, the `sai-workflow` schema, and the proposal template;
  - the `/sai-1-spec`, backfill, and Direct Build consumers.
- Out of scope: design.md, tasks.md, implementation.md — not generated by /sai-backfill

## Request Additional Notes

Motivating case: in an opencode session, a crystallized block for an unrelated project omitted this field. Detailed future behavior discussed during exploration (edit and rename rules, transfer to another owner, maintenance assignment, a future authorization gate, a related follow-up PBI, and one undecided point) was lost, and only terse non-goals survived in `Key constraints`. The same block also placed a future requirement ("the request must be recorded as Pending…") in `Key constraints`. The user had to ask for the notes explicitly.

Future scope, outside this change: `writing-for-agents` is a user-global skill that this repository does not ship, and no opencode projection covers it. Whether SAI should ship it, or cite it SAI-wide for every instruction-editing implementer, is a separate decision.
