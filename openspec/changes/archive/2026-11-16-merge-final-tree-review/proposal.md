## Why

In both observed rebases of v0.12.0-beta, the three real integration defects (a duplicated paragraph, an outdated duplicated test, and a duplicated spec requirement) sat outside every conflict region, and one came from a commit replayed without conflicts that no review step of `/sai-merge` covered. No duplicate check existed beyond the single-Summary lint. `/sai-merge` therefore reviewed only where no defect was found and never looked where every defect was.

## What Changes

- The `/sai-merge` coordinator gains a **Final-tree review** step in its Final section, before the suite runs: it reviews the diff of the final tree against the source branch over every file the run's commits touched, including commits replayed without conflicts and a launch without conflicts, and judges it against the commit intent of both sides. Each finding and judgment is written to `final-review.md` in the run directory, which a resumed run reads.
- `sai/tools/merge.js` gains a `duplicates` subcommand: from the captured provenance, it compares the final tree (the index) with both sides over the files that differ from both, ignores lines with no letter or digit, and lists each block of 4 or more significant lines that appears at least twice in the final tree and at most once in the same file on each side, with the `path:line`, byte range, and `before_hash` of every occurrence. Binary, non-UTF-8, and too-large files are reported as skipped; the result is capped at 100 blocks. The tool flags and never decides.
- Each flagged block is judged: an intentional repetition stays with its reason, and a defect is corrected. Corrections go through the new worker step `sai/commands/merge/steps/final-correction.md`, captured with `merge.js correction` and written by the worker's `apply` step through `merge.js write`; the coordinator writes no repository file itself.
- The merge worker is also created when a final correction is needed and none exists, so a clean launch or an all-shortcut run creates no worker unless the final review needs a correction.
- Final corrections follow the test-correction commit rule: on a merge they join the merge commit, and on a rebase they go in one additional local commit.
- A finding still open after 3 attempts is the new obstacle `final-review`.
- The closing report lists the final-tree review with each introduced duplicate block, its occurrences, and what was done about it.
- `sai/commands/merge/mechanics.md`, `sai/policies/worker-report-transport.md` (merge role fields), `docs/commands/sai-merge.md`, and `AGENTS.md` document the new step, subcommand, and report content. Tests cover the duplicate detection and the updated coordinator card.

Scope drift (not an implementation error): the `final-review` obstacle name and its 3-attempt budget, the `final-review.md` run-directory record used for resuming, and the skipped-file reasons and 100-block cap of `merge.js duplicates`. The selected diff evidences these behaviors, but the supplied intent statement did not name them.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None

### Modified Capabilities

- `sai-merge-command`: final-tree review scope, introduced-duplicate detection, final-tree correction handling, worker creation for a final correction, and closing-report content for introduced duplicate blocks.
- `merge-worker-discipline`: the `final-correction` step joins the steps a merge worker receives by pointer.
