> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

Any non-empty intent advanced explore-idea@1, reproduced with banana intent, and the caller-side advancement rule cited by slice.md still pointed at a deleted rule, leaving nothing defining when advancing is allowed. This change hardens both layers so only explicit next-step advancement moves the progression while preserving recorded-list semantics.

## What Changes

- sai-state/machines/explore-idea.js — replaces any-non-empty hasIntent with an exact next-step allowlist (ADVANCE_INTENT plus isAdvanceIntent); any other, missing, or empty intent stays put with rejected READINESS_IS_NOT_INTENT and unchanged state; recordedList recording and empty-list auto-advance on a later no-intent emit are unchanged.
- sai/commands/explore/steps/common.md — restores the caller-side next-step recognition rule: bare token with optional trivial punctuation or greeting, or dominant intent naming the next stage, requesting crystallization, or semantic confirmation at list stages; mere containment never fires and negate, defer, quote, or discuss never advances.
- test/explore-idea-intent-gate.test.js — adds intent-gate coverage: banana, missing, and empty intents reject without advancing; next-step advances exactly one stage per emit through crystallize; recordedList records without advancing with empty-list auto-advance only on a later no-intent emit; common.md recognition text and slice.md pointer verified.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None

### Modified Capabilities

- `explore-stage-machine`: explore-idea machine advances only on the exact next-step intent with in-band rejection otherwise, preserving recordedList and empty-list auto-advance behavior.
- `explore-pre-crystallization-stages`: caller-side next-step recognition restored in common.md with bare-token and dominant-intent firing rules and containment, negate, defer, quote, and discuss exclusions.

## Additional Notes

- Both layers are fixed together: machine allowlist plus prose restore; either alone leaves one layer unguarded.
- next-step stays the single advancement intent; reaching crystallize remains the third next-step, not a new intent.
- Strict allowlist rejects future intent names until explicitly added; accepted because silent advancement is worse than explicit rejection.
- Rejected alternatives (machine-only without prose restore; prose-only without machine hardening) were considered and rejected for leaving the caller or the wire unguarded.
