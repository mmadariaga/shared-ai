## Why

A supervised Plan-Unattended run stopped after the spec worker returned completed and presented the next slice, skipping the contractually required chained design dispatch for the same change. The spec-to-design transition needed a machine-enforced authority so a run cannot close after sai-1 without dispatching sai-2.

## What Changes

- sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-plan-unattended.md — Added the Plan-Unattended chain guard paragraph declaring explore-slice@1 the chain authority, requiring the mandatory complete emit (sai-1 to sai-2) before design dispatch and turn close, validating stage before every dispatch, forbidding close after Supervised sai-1 done while stage is still sai-1, blocking the next-slice selector and new route dispatches while stage is sai-1 or sai-2, keeping supervision state conversation-only, and holding the stage with explicit instruction on store failure or rejected emit.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- plan-unattended-chain-guard: explore-slice@1 enforces the spec-to-design chain, concurrency blocking, and degraded-store hold for Plan-Unattended runs.

### Modified Capabilities

None.
