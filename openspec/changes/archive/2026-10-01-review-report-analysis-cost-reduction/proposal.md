**Complexity**: high (12 capabilities, 38 requirement deltas)

## Why

Mutation analysis frequently added engine probing and unavailable-result boilerplate without distinct findings. Review also duplicated findings and recommendations across report sections; useful domain and resilience analysis can remain without that presentation overhead.

## What Changes

- Remove review Pass 12, its machine state, instruction file, engine probing, mutation outcomes, identifiers, severity handling, and adversary exemption. Keep passes 1–11 and the Testing pass.
- Use four review progress steps and three complete-path events, with resolution and scope reported together. Keep coordinator-owned state and separate just-in-time step delivery; other audits retain five steps and four events.
- **BREAKING** Remove Next Steps, Domain Alignment Check, Resilience Surface Triage, and Mutation Analysis from both review templates. Summary records goal coverage and scope creep in one or two lines without duplicating findings; decision contradictions remain findings.
- Record the resilience outcome, including no surface, affected paths, and relevant notes in Coverage Notes. Preserve resilience findings, ownership and severity rules, all three audit recommendations, and the closing tally.
- Keep Stryker as independent development tooling. Retire the review smoke runner, three fixtures, smoke script, and managed mutation instruction in both harnesses.
- Reconcile affected specification contracts and tests. Add ADR 0190 superseding 0012 and amending 0107b without rewriting historical records; already-superseded 0013 and 0106b remain unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `independent-mutation-development-tool`: standalone Stryker configuration and script, with review smoke machinery absent.

### Modified Capabilities

- `audit-command-progress-plans`: four review steps and three events; other audit plans preserved.
- `instruction-output-templates`: simplified review structure with command-owned write-time authority.
- `mutation-analysis`: retire every requirement and remove the capability during archive.
- `resilience-check`: move resilience recording into Coverage Notes.
- `resilience-pass`: retain pass 11 and its findings without a mutation successor.
- `review-phase-worker`: passes 1–11, report-only writes, simplified report verification, and unchanged adversarial checks except the removed mutation exemption.
- `review-standalone-loading`: four-state review routing and just-in-time pointers.
- `review-test-correctness`: assertions for eleven passes, four steps, and three events.
- `review-worker-bindings`: read-only research without mutation delegation.
- `step-machine-wiring-verification`: exact coordinator/machine alignment.
- `worker-active-step-execution`: analysis proceeds directly to close without a replacement gate.
