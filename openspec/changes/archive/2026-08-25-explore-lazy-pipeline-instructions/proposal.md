> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

Every `/sai-explore` invocation previously loaded the complete 156,413-byte instruction contract, including behavior that most sessions never reached. The implemented split reduces startup delivery to 26,404 bytes while preserving the remaining 130,780 bytes as deferred, trigger-reachable instruction content.

## What Changes

The monolithic `sai/commands/explore/instructions.md` contract is reduced to an 11,313-byte nucleus plus a direct fetch of `steps/common.md` containing 15,091 bytes of shared rules. Ten named step files now carry the deferred artifact-review, slicing, crystallization, review-loop, selector, supervised, auto-fast, and idea-list contracts. Fetch directives are placed after the complete sentence or contract paragraph they follow, and transitive Fetch reachability is tested.

The selector keeps its existing Auto, Auto (fast implementation), and Manual semantics. The supervised route and the eight-step auto-fast route remain in fixed order. The Claude Code and opencode idea-list renderers are updated to reference the split instruction surfaces while retaining their harness-specific panel carriers.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `explore-instruction-delivery`: Deliver the explore instruction contract through a small startup nucleus and ten conditionally fetched, transitively reachable step files while preserving the deferred contract text.

### Modified Capabilities

- `explore-pipeline-selector`: Preserve the existing three-option selector and its supervised and auto-fast route behavior after moving the selector and route contracts into reachable step files.

## Impact

- `sai/commands/explore/instructions.md` becomes the startup nucleus and retains the read-only boundary, code-graph check, staged progression, shared close, and Phase A panel ownership.
- New lazy instruction files are added under `sai/commands/explore/steps/`: `common.md`, `artifact-review-language-gate.md`, `slicing-assessment.md`, `crystallization-language-gates.md`, `crystallization-protocol.md`, `review-loop.md`, `pipeline-selector.md`, `pipeline-auto-supervised.md`, `pipeline-auto-fast.md`, and `idea-list.md`.
- `sai/adapters/claude/idea-list-render.md` and `sai/adapters/opencode/idea-list-render.md` are updated to reference the split instruction surfaces while preserving their harness-specific carriers.
- Contract, reachability, installation, renderer, selector, pre-crystallization, overview, and supervised-path tests are updated or added to read the split source set.
- Startup instruction delivery is 26,404 bytes; deferred instruction delivery is 130,780 bytes.
- No Slice 2 or Slice 3 behavior is implemented.
- Out of scope: design.md, tasks.md, implementation.md — not generated by /sai-backfill
