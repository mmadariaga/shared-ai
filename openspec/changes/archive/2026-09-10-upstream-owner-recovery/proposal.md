## Why

Rule 8's recorded rationale in bounded-recovery is warm-journal reuse — "a replacement has an empty journal by contract and cannot perform the repair that depends on the live worker's dispatch state" — not a prohibition on cross-role feedback. The in-scope/out-of-scope split conflates instance identity with role identity, so every cross-role cause routes to a human even when the owning worker is warm and inside the same run. The same dead-end recurs in at least three lanes; fixing each locally would triple the casuistry and leave the vocabulary wrong.

## What Changes

Four files in the orchestration and recovery surface are modified to support upstream-worker recovery continuation:

- `sai/policies/bounded-recovery.md`: Adds a fourth Cause Locus value `owner-in-run` to represent causes located in artifacts owned by another still-resumable worker of the same run. Modifies rule 4 to define the locus and its ownership test. Modifies rules 6-7 for eligibility; narrows out-of-scope to "no in-run owner holds the boundary". Modifies rule 8 to target the continuation to "the live worker holding the authorized correction boundary" rather than always the same worker, while preserving the no-replacement prohibition verbatim. Adds "Downstream relaunch after owner correction" section defining the relaunch as a warm continuation carrying corrected upstream artifacts.

- `sai/orchestration/command-runner.md`: Clarifies that a worker's `completed` status closes its dispatch phase but does not end its resumability for recovery purposes.

- `sai/commands/design/coordinator.md`: Routes recovery to the upstream (spec) worker when Cause Locus is `owner-in-run`, marking that recovery as the first consumer for a prior-phase cause.

- `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-plan-unattended.md`: Modifies the "never re-dispatch sai-1" prohibition to allow bounded-recovery `owner-in-run` diagnosis routing.
