> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

A Plan - Unattended slice close, and the sliced Manual hand-off, send the user to another chat (to run `/sai-build` or `/sai-1-spec`) and back to the explore chat with `next-slice`. By the time the user returns, the explore chat's prompt cache has usually expired, so the next slice resumes cold. No explore step armed main-session cache warming at that idle point, although the self-warming mechanism (`wait.js warm` with its chain) already existed.

## What Changes

- New step file `sai/commands/explore/steps/handoff-warming.md` single-sources the hand-off warming behavior: it runs as the last step of a successful Plan - Unattended close and of a sliced Manual hand-off, and only when at least one other slice is still pending.
- The step resolves the `warming` project setting for the `agent` role with `project-settings.js warming --role agent`: an applicable entry supplies the interval and uses `max` as the duration with no question; an off entry means no warming and no question; `origin: none` (or a failed lookup) leads to the question.
- The question prints a harness cache-lifetime (TTL) notice and asks one native picker call with two questions: interval (`30 min (Recommended)`, `4 min`, `No`) and duration (`4h (Recommended)`, `2h`, `1h`), both accepting free text. `No` or a dismissed picker means no warming; an unanswered duration is `4h`; an invalid answer is asked once more and then dropped with a one-line note. The first answer applies to every later hand-off close of the same invocation.
- The wait is armed once, at the end of the hand-off close, with `wait.js warm` and a chain; only a wake re-arms it, replacing the held background task id. Real messages other than `next-slice` neither re-arm it nor move the deadline. Arming failure is reported in one line and the close continues unchanged.
- `next-slice` (in `slice.md` and the Plan `next-slice` rule of `pipeline-plan-unattended.md`) and a new `/sai-explore` invocation in the same chat (`body.md`) stop the wait through the cache-warming policy's "Stop your own wait". The answers, chain, and task id live in conversation state only.
- `sai/policies/cache-warming.md` gains a `## Hand-off wait` section: fixed deadline counted from the arm, arm once, wake re-arms with the returned state and the new task id, no setting resolution on a wake when the interval came from the user's answers, and state kept in the conversation only. "Stop your own wait" now names the hand-off wait and states that stopping an already-ended task is harmless.
- The `explore-command` capability profile gains `stopTask: true`; `bin/capabilities.js` adds the `stopTask` flag, which projects `TaskStop` on Claude Code and adds no opencode rule (opencode stops the task by removing its shell).
- `docs/commands/sai-explore.md` and `docs/project-settings.md` document the hand-off warming and the `agent`-role setting lookup.
- Tests cover the step file, its two loaders, the stops, the policy section, and the `TaskStop` projection; the explore step-file count moves from 17 to 18.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `explore-handoff-warming`: when, at an explore hand-off close, the session warms its own cache; where the timings come from; the question; the fixed-deadline arm; and the stops.

### Modified Capabilities

- `agent-self-warming`: a hand-off wait variant with a fixed deadline and no re-arm on real messages, and a harmless stop of an already-ended wait.
- `contract-tool-capabilities`: the `explore-command` profile may stop the session's own background task, projected as `TaskStop` on Claude Code.
