## Why

GREEN sees the step's tests and can satisfy them without meeting the spec. Before this change, `/sai-3-implement-new` only read each step's diff against its brief, and the documentation said the slice had no review round after GREEN. A per-step adversarial review catches a test-gamed or out-of-scope implementation before later steps build on it. It reuses the criteria of the Direct Build Step 2 functional fix loop (`sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md`) instead of defining a new review.

## What Changes

- The **Review** stage of `sai/commands/implement-new/coordinator.md` becomes an adversarial review after GREEN. The coordinator reads the step's diff (`git diff HEAD`) against the brief's spec scenarios and tasks, with two checks:
  - a structural check: every scenario and task maps to production paths and to an asserting test, the tests are not gamed, nothing beyond the brief or anticipatory is added, and the production files match the plan or a deviation justifies them;
  - an empirical check: the coordinator exercises the behavior through the project's runnable surface with the executor whenever it can.
- The review is limited to spec fidelity, test gaming, and excess scope. Style, design, performance, and general security are left to the later review command.
- The coordinator fetches `sai/policies/artifact-review-contract.md` and forms findings in its shape. Scope observations are neither fixed nor recorded as pending. Each round's findings go to `reviews/step-{n}-round-{k}.md` in the run directory.
- Findings go as one ordered list to the worker that owns the defect: GREEN for production code, RED for a wrong test. A finding about a scenario that has no test goes through RED first: the new test must fail by assertion and is staged before GREEN fixes the code. After each fix the step's tests run again, the GREEN closing check is re-applied, and the changed files join the step's paths.
- Fix rounds are not attempts. There are at most 3 rounds. Findings that survive the third round are not a failure, not an obstacle, and not escalated: they go to `findings.md`, the coordinator emits `record` (`finding`) the first time that file is written, and the step commits. A worker that fails to carry out a fix round is an ordinary obstacle of the step.
- `docs/commands/sai-3-implement-new.md` describes the adversarial review step and the pending findings in `build-log.md`, and drops the statement that the slice has no review round after GREEN.
- `test/implement-new-family.test.js` allows the new contract fetch and asserts the review's criteria, scope, rounds, and pending-finding handling.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `implement-new-adversarial-review`: the post-GREEN review of each step's diff against its spec scenarios and tasks, with bounded fix rounds and pending findings carried to the run log.

### Modified Capabilities

- `implement-new-loop`: the coordinator also loads the shared artifact review contract, and the per-step review now follows the adversarial review instead of counting each defect as an attempt.
- `implement-new-resilience`: a step review fix round is not an attempt of the per-obstacle budget.

## Impact

- Modified: `sai/commands/implement-new/coordinator.md`, `docs/commands/sai-3-implement-new.md`, `test/implement-new-family.test.js`.
- New files: none.
- Some findings may survive the run as pending entries in `findings.md` and `build-log.md` instead of being fixed.
- Out of scope: design.md, tasks.md, implementation.md — not generated by /sai-backfill
