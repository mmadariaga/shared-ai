> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why
Picker questions often overflowed the visible area, hiding options and forcing scroll. Splitting full decision context into preceding plain text while keeping the picker question and options to a couple of lines restores scannability without losing information.

## What Changes
- sai/policies/question-context.md now defines a universal concise-format rule with mandatory split, emission order, operational limits, self-sufficient preceding text, universal scope with no exemptions, verbatim literals, and no-picker fallback. The pinned exemption registry was removed and manual review is the verification method with lint deferred.
- sai/commands/backfill/instructions.md and worker.md shorten the change-name proposal to a short summary question with preceding plain-text context.
- sai/commands/commit/instructions.md and worker.md reference the concise-format rule for the commit authorization prompt and carry secret-file and already-pushed amend context in preceding plain text.
- sai/commands/explore/steps/route-selector.md keeps the crystallization-close selector on the native picker with Ready to Propose blocks as preceding context, a short summary question, bounded option descriptions, and a shortened Manual option.
- sai/policies/artifact-feedback-gate.md, change-picker.md, status-picker.md, and remember.md add concise-format prefaces or cross-references and shorten long prompts to comply with the limits.
- Constraints preserved: no new deterministic validator in this change with manual review only, no change to picker mechanics in remember.md beyond a cross-reference, and no OpenSpec artifact format change beyond prompt wording.
- Limitations carried forward: rewriting all violating prompts in the same change increases diff size for consistent experience, and Spanish text expands about 20 to 30 percent versus English forcing tighter summaries in Spanish.
