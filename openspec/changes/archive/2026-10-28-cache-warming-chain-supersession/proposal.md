> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

Cache-warming waits accumulated: a real message armed a new wait while the earlier wait kept re-arming, so one agent could hold several live waits. A wake's `ok` reply had no dispatcher handling, an agent that armed after its closing report or terminal result kept waking a finished session, and the `explore-command` capability profile did not allow `/sai-explore` to run the cache-warmer wait script. The benchmark's warming override also left waits armed before it running with the old timings.

## What Changes

- `skills/universal/sai-cache-warmer/wait.js` gains a `chain` subcommand that prints a fresh chain id without reading standard input. An agent runs it once before its first arm and passes the id with every arm that follows a real message.
- The `warm` state carries the chain id, a generation, and the arm instant. A first `warm` call bumps the chain's generation in a private chain record under the harness's SAI temp root, resolved through `sai/tools/temp-root.js`. A wait whose generation is no longer current returns `finished` at its wake, whether it was superseded before or during its sleep, so each agent keeps a single live wait. Chains of different agents are independent. Chain ids must be 16 lowercase hexadecimal characters. A chain record is released when its current wait finishes, and records older than 24 hours are pruned.
- On every wake, `wait.js` reads the cutoff mark `warming-cutoff.json` under the SAI temp root and ends any wait armed before it.
- `skills/universal/sai-context-cache-ttl-benchmark/warming_override.js` writes that cutoff mark after `apply` and after both `restore` outcomes, moving it only forward. `apply` records whether it created `.sai/`, and `restore` removes `.sai/` only when the override created it and it is empty.
- `sai/policies/cache-warming.md` defines the chain and the final response. It treats a subagent message that is exactly `ok` as a wake acknowledgement, never a result, with no attempt spent. It stops arming after a final response (a closing report or a terminal result) while keeping it after a question or an intermediate step report, and describes the `chain` call and the passing of the chain id on arm and wake. Its Window section states the cutoff's reach: every chain armed before it, of every agent, including sessions of other projects on the same harness.
- `sai/install-manifest.json` adds `node {skills}/sai-cache-warmer/wait.js *` to the `explore-command` shell list, in the same form `cache-warmer-command` uses; `bin/capabilities.js` (`resolveProfile`) is unchanged.
- `docs/project-settings.md` and `docs/commands/sai-context-cache-ttl-benchmark.md` describe the single live chain, the absence of arming after a final response, the cutoff and its reach, and the `.sai/` folder removal.
- `test/cache-warmer.test.js` and `test/context-cache-ttl-benchmark.test.js` cover chain supersession, per-agent chains, the cutoff, record pruning and release, the `chain` subcommand, the explore grant on both harnesses, the policy wording, and the `.sai/` removal.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `agent-self-warming`: no arming after a final response, a single live wait chain per agent, cutoff-aware wakes, and wake acknowledgement handling by dispatchers.
- `benchmark-warming-override`: the override writes a forward-only warming cutoff mark and removes the `.sai/` folder it created when that folder is empty.
- `contract-tool-capabilities`: the `explore-command` profile grants the cache-warmer wait script.
