> **⚠ POST-HOC RECORD** — This proposal was backfilled after implementation against a user-supplied statement of intent. It describes a decision already made, not one being proposed.

## Why

`sai/policies/backlog-implementation-start.md` gave no prior assignment authorization to a new slice, so every slice that the Direct Build automatic chain started asked the assignment question again. This contradicted the set-level pre-authorization that the `per-slice` branch answer already gives to chained branch switches, and it stopped an unattended chain at every slice. Round 1 of the Explore route selector also left the assignment question out when the issue did not exist yet, even when the backlog answer would create it.

## What Changes

- `sai/commands/explore/steps/route-selector.md`: the round 1 assignment question is asked once for the whole `last_crystallization_set`, and its answer is recorded as the set-level assignment answer that applies to the issue of every slice in the set. When no issue is linked, the question is asked in an intent form (options `assign` and `skip` only, no named user) when the backlog answer is Yes, and left out when the backlog answer is No. The answer is reset, together with the overview answer, when a new crystallization replaces the set. An intent answer lapses, like an approval, when its publication is cancelled or fails. The Direct Build continuation `yes` states that later-slice assignment stays authorized only by the set-level assignment answer.
- `sai/policies/backlog-implementation-start.md`: a new **Set-level answer** section makes an explicit exception to the rule that a new slice receives no prior assignment authorization. The answer covers every later slice of the set, is not consumed by the first slice, and is reused whether the slice starts through the automatic chain or a later route picker answer. `skip` performs no remote operation for every slice; `assign` and an intent assign when the issue has no assignees or only the approved user, and stop to ask keep, replace, or skip when the issue has other assignees; `keep` and `replace` apply only when the current assignees equal the approved context. A changed identity or a failed assignment follows the existing Retry/Skip/Stop steps: Retry and Skip continue the chain, Stop ends it. An intent answer binds to the authenticated user at the first implementation start.
- `sai/commands/explore/steps/pipeline-direct-build.md`: Step 1 applies the set's assignment answer without a question for a slice started by `auto_continue`, asks any question the policy raises, and keeps the chain open across it.
- `test/explore-question-rounds.test.js`: assertions updated for the intent form, the set-level answer, and the new policy section.
