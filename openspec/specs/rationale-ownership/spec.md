## Requirements
### Requirement: Agreed trade-offs SHALL be recorded in the block rationale fields

Explore SHALL record the agreed trade-offs in the block's Decisions & Rationale and Trade-offs Accepted fields.

#### Scenario: Trade-off lands in rationale fields
- **WHEN** a trade-off is agreed during exploration
- **THEN** explore records it in Decisions & Rationale and Trade-offs Accepted

### Requirement: Agreed WHY SHALL be recorded in Why plus Key constraints

Explore SHALL record the agreed WHY in the block's Why plus Key constraints fields.

#### Scenario: WHY lands in Why and constraints
- **WHEN** the shared WHY is agreed during exploration
- **THEN** explore records it in Why plus Key constraints
