  **Direct Build (unattended) supervision contract**: The third selector option runs one unattended code-first flow for the currently selected slice, ending with an archived, committed change in one session. A sliced crystallization set is therefore handled as a sequence of independently completed slice runs: each run enters through the existing selector and executes the same fixed worker order, while the completion transition below decides whether another slice-selection turn is needed. A single-slice run retains its existing terminal behavior. It composes exclusively existing contracts — the three-round budget semantics, **Bounded Recovery**, the archive fast-track gates, and `@sai/policies/commit-rules.md` faithfulness rules — and exists beside, never instead of, the Plan (unattended) contract above; its stable route identity is `direct-build-unattended`. Selecting it consents delegated writes (item 1) AND pre-authorizes the local commit; it is consent to selection and dispatch only.

   **`direct-build-unattended` route projection lifecycle.** The selected slice's temporary Build route is initialized before Step 1 and is rendered by the explore coordinator as exactly three high-level items: `Build/Implement`, `Backfill`, and `Archive`. `Build/Implement` starts `in_progress`; after the implementer and functional fix loop converge, it becomes `completed` and `Backfill` becomes `in_progress`; after clean backfill execution, `Backfill` becomes `completed` and `Archive` becomes `in_progress`; after the final archive execution and pre-authorized commit return a clean terminal `completed`, `Archive` becomes `completed`. The route label `Build/Implement` is not `/sai-build` and does not mean that the `/sai-build` or `meta-build` composition ran.

   The eight internal Build-route steps remain authoritative, but functional review, spec review, ADR/DDR checks, preparation, execution, and commit are substeps of these three route items and never become separate panel entries. The route is not a routed `progress_plan`; it is the coordinator-only idea-list projection for the selected slice and replaces that slice's baseline evidence entries without duplicating them. A failed, cancelled, STOP-bearing, coordinator-disproved, or unrecovered result leaves the active high-level route item `pending`, preserves earlier completed route items, starts no later item, and leaves the slice retryable. Only the clean terminal archive result completes the route. A new slice selection creates fresh route state for that slice; completed slices and pending slices retain their independent states.
