# Review edge cases (stage 2)

The shared presentation contract is **Display and question** in `list-writing.md`; answer evaluation, presentation currency, and the `- None` rule live in **List stages** in `steps/common.md`.

**Edge-case review gate (stage 2 — before readiness and crystallization).** The edge-case review is the second stage of the staged progression. It runs when the user advances into `Review edge cases`. The review state is conversation-only and contains the ordered proposed list and its agreement state. The review runs at most once while the idea remains substantially unchanged.

- **Terminology agreement (pre-edge-cases, conversation-only).** Before the edge-case review, fix ambiguous terms in conversation. Agreed terms travel as the block's `Terms`. Explore performs no file writes; it never writes `GLOSSARY.md`.
- **The list.** Fetch @sai/commands/explore/list-writing.md and follow it for the draft, review pass, and display sequence and the unavailable-skill warning. When the idea came through issue intake with a carried `E` list, that list is the starting list; add items only from this stage's checks. Only include behaviors that define a behavior or scope boundary for this proposed change. Do not invent `E` items from unrelated possibilities; cases outside the change remain Non-Goals. Number the proposed behaviors consecutively as `E1` through `En`, then run the lightweight probes, then apply **Display and question** in `list-writing.md`. The emission is one message: the formal list first, then the probes, then the question — nothing sits between them. An empty list (`- None`) runs no probes.
- **Lightweight probe (post-list sub-step, conversation-only).** After the formal edge-case list has been drafted and before the agreement question, systematically probe candidate scenarios against the proposed list. Exercise at least 2 scenarios before anything is shown; holes a probe uncovers are fixed in the list privately, so the list is published already coherent, with no change marks and no explanations. The review pass from `list-writing.md` runs after these private corrections and before the one-message emission. Only material probes are shown: a probe is material when its outcome adds, removes, or modifies an `E` item, or clarifies a non-obvious consequence of an existing one. The search stops when no remaining scenario is material; the floor is a search duty, not a quota to pad with noise. When fewer than 2 material scenarios exist, show what the probes produced and state explicitly that there are no more material scenarios. Each shown probe is a plain direct question in the ambient conversation language — no fixed formula — immediately followed by a self-sufficient prose answer whose `E(N)` citations trail at the end as reference. Probes serve only this change's edge-case list; they do not open Implementation details or crystallization decisions. The probe does not replace the formal list.
- **Feedback ripples.** Explanations of side effects are owed only when user feedback drags changes into `E` items other than the one the feedback addressed (ripples); feedback aimed at a shown probe binds to the `E` item that probe illustrates.
