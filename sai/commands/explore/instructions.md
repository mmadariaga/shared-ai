You are in explore mode — a read-and-discuss context. These rules hold for the entire session, except while the exploration is paused or closed (rule 4):

1. **No file writes**: This command MUST NOT create, modify, or delete files — read, search, and discuss only. 2. **Research diagnostics**: When a `budget-explorer` result carries `ladder_discards`, Fetch `@sai/policies/explore-agent.md` and apply only § Missing-tool notice presentation to the result. Retain the complete diagnostics internally; do not adopt the explorer role or its shell restrictions.

3. **Direct-looking request classification.** Treat an input phrased as a direct implementation command — a `direct-looking request` (e.g. "Implement …", "Fix …", "Add …", "Apply …") — as the initial content of a candidate idea, never as authorization to implement, dispatch, or leave Explore. 4. **Leave confirmation.** On every turn in which no route of the current slice is running, the session leaves the explore flow only after the user picks **Stay**, **Pause**, or **Close** in a native leave selector. That covers the stages from invocation start through the wait for the route selector answer, and again each time `explore-slice@1` is back in `waiting` with the route selector presented for a later or a parked slice.    - **Leaving** is any step that takes the session out of the explore flow: a user message whose dominant intent is to stop exploring or to do other work than the idea under exploration (recognized as `next-step` is: a side question answered inside explore, a quote, a negation, or a mere mention is not leaving), your own proposal to go elsewhere, or a skill whose close proposes or requires going elsewhere.    - **Selector.** Before the request that leads out runs, present one closed-choice question through the harness-native picker (`AskUserQuestion` on Claude Code, `question` on opencode) in the chat language, with the options `Stay` (`stay`), `Pause` (`pause`), and `Close` (`close`), in that order.    - **Stay** changes nothing and does not run the request: ask the pending question again, or show the turn closure of `steps/common.md`.
   - **Pause.** Emit `--set intent=pause` to `explore-idea@1` per `@sai/policies/stage-machine.md`.    - **While paused or closed**, the turn is not an explore turn (`body.md` § Load receipt): apply no explore contract and load nothing for it. A paused exploration treats explore tokens (`next-step`, `review-loop`, `poc-lane`, `goto`, and the others) as ordinary text.
